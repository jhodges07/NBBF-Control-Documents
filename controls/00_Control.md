==================================================
GOVERNING AUTHORITY
==================================================

This document is the supreme CONTROL of the Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF).

All other NBBF CONTROL documents, nodes, actors, and derivative systems
derive their legitimacy from this document.

No CONTROL, budget, policy, process, or execution artifact may exist
outside the authority granted herein.

Where conflict exists among NBBF documents, this document prevails.

This CONTROL establishes constitutional-engineering purpose, authority
hierarchy, governing principles, interpretation rules, and fail-closed locks.

This CONTROL does not own detailed subordinate operating mechanics.
It is not an operating-policy manual.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

The purpose of NBBF is to engineer lawful republican government so that
public authority remains constitutional, human-accountable, measurable,
transparent, auditable, reviewable, recoverable, and aligned with taxpayer
interests.

NBBF preserves accountability from legislative authorization to the lowest
human exercising operational judgment.

This document also establishes:

• The source of lawful authority within NBBF
• The hierarchy of CONTROL documents
• The rules governing publication, enforcement, inheritance, and replacement
• The conditions under which authority may be exercised, limited, or revoked

This document does not prescribe subordinate operating procedures.

==================================================
2. WHAT NBBF IS AND IS NOT
==================================================

NBBF is a constitutional-engineering specification.

It defines how lawful republican governments organize, authorize, execute,
measure, review, audit, and terminate public authority.

NBBF is not:

• A political party
• A legislative proposal
• A budget
• A software application
• An accounting system
• A campaign platform
• A replacement for constitutional government

==================================================
3. JURISDICTION
==================================================

NBBF is jurisdiction-neutral.

The same engineering principles apply at every lawful republican level of
government.

Detailed republican-government jurisdiction structure belongs to CONTROL 03.

Recognition that school districts and other lawful governing bodies may
operate as republics explains jurisdiction neutrality only.

It does not establish NBEF authority.

==================================================
4. SIBLING AND IMPLEMENTATION SYSTEMS
==================================================

NBBF complements independent related specifications and implementations.

AGCL addresses constitutional philosophy, taxpayer property rights, authority
limitations, monetary principles, and liberty-centered governance as its own
authority.

The Constitutional Digital Twin (CDT) may digitally represent NBBF structures.

UNBKE may provide knowledge engineering, evidence management, traceability,
and runtime knowledge support.

These systems remain independent. NBBF does not absorb their authority.
An AGCL CONTROL is not an NBBF CONTROL.

Specialized domain frameworks MAY exist outside this repository.

NBBF MAY provide foundational governmental budgeting and accountability
principles used by such frameworks.

A specialized domain framework SHALL NOT become an NBBF CONTROL merely
because it interoperates with or derives principles from NBBF.

Specialized domain frameworks MAY maintain separate repositories, CONTROL
trees, governance, versions, and implementations.

Interoperability SHALL NOT silently transfer authority between frameworks.

NBBF SHALL NOT absorb specialized domain doctrine unless separately
authorized through the NBBF CONTROL architecture.

NBEF is the anticipated education-domain example of such a framework.
NBEF is not an NBBF CONTROL. This CONTROL does not create NBEF, establish
education mechanics, or assign NBEF an NBBF CONTROL number.

CDT, UNBKE, artificial intelligence, APIs, ledgers, runtimes, or other digital
implementations SHALL NOT replace human accountability, lawful human judgment,
or constitutional authority.

CONTROL 00C owns NBBF property-rights and pooling operating doctrine.

  CONTROL

Canonical CONTROLs SHALL declare:

• Exit authority

11. ENGINEERING AXIOMS
==================================================

The following axioms are Root principles. They are not subordinate operating
procedures.

AXIOM 1 — Reality disciplines authority.

AXIOM 2 — Every lawful level of government functions as a republic.

AXIOM 3 — Drift is the first enemy of liberty.

AXIOM 4 — Authority exists to accomplish a defined public purpose.

AXIOM 5 — Every exercise of public authority SHALL terminate in an identifiable
human owner capable of reasonably understanding, supervising, defending, and
answering for that responsibility. Authority SHALL recursively decompose until
reaching the lowest human capable of directly exercising operational judgment.

AXIOM 6 — Every human owner serves as both executor and guardian of public
trust.

AXIOM 7 — Failure SHALL become visible while still small enough to correct.

AXIOM 8 — Structure precedes measurement. Measurement precedes management.

AXIOM 9 — Knowledge is distributed. NBBF preserves lawful authority while
allowing operational knowledge to travel upward through protected signals.

AXIOM 10 — Structure is superior to personalities.

AXIOM 11 — Transparency is a system function.

AXIOM 12 — Every public dollar must answer for purpose, ownership, and result.

==================================================
12. SIX PILLARS
==================================================

NBBF is founded upon six Root organizing principles.

Root states the principle. Detailed operating authority belongs to the
identified CONTROL. Missing subordinate content SHALL NOT be invented here.

PILLAR 1 — Taxpayer Supremacy
Government exists to faithfully steward taxpayer resources.
Operating boundaries: CONTROL 00C.

PILLAR 2 — Human Accountability
Every public responsibility terminates in one accountable human owner.
Operating boundaries: CONTROL 01.

PILLAR 3 — Signals
Reality always possesses a protected path back to authority.
Operating authority: CONTROL 00B.

PILLAR 4 — Localized and Recoverable Failure
Failure SHALL become visible, measurable, localized, and recoverable.
Where failure is called educational, it means failure produces knowledge,
feedback, and system learning. It does not establish NBEF.
Operating authority: CONTROL 00D, CONTROL 07, and CONTROL 08 as applicable.

PILLAR 5 — Digital Republic
Every lawful republic SHOULD be capable of faithful digital representation
without altering its constitutional structure.
Operating authority: CONTROL 11.

PILLAR 6 — Continuous Stewardship and Reauthorization
Authority, budgets, public resources, and responsibilities SHALL undergo
continuous operational review and periodic legislative reauthorization.
Operating authority: CONTROL 05 and CONTROL 10.
