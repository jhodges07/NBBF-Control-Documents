==================================================
NBBF 07.1. Purpose
==================================================

The Audit and Performance Standards define how Nodes are evaluated,
challenged, and corrected under the Node-Based Budget Framework, and how
audit findings may inform authorized exit or recovery review.

Audits under NBBF are continuous, structural, and consequence-bearing.
They exist to preserve taxpayer supremacy, not to generate reports.

Key rules:
• Continuous audit, not episodic theater
• Performance is measurable
• Failure produces consequences

Operating Exit/Disengagement: CONTROL 00D.
This CONTROL does not own Node termination procedure.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.1

==================================================
NBBF 07.2. Audit Is Structural, Not Investigative
==================================================

Auditing under NBBF is embedded in system design.

Audits are driven by:
• Node structure
• Signals and variance
• Declared scope and ceilings

Audits do not depend on whistleblowers, press exposure,
or political initiative.

Key rules:
• Structure triggers audit
• No discretionary initiation
• No selective enforcement

This CONTROL MAY receive, inspect, verify, or audit matters surfaced
through authorized Signals. Signal operating rules: CONTROL 00B.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.2

==================================================
NBBF 07.3. Continuous Performance Measurement
==================================================

All Nodes are measured continuously against:
• Declared scope
• Authorized cost
• Delivered outcomes
• Time and readiness constraints

Cost and Value measures used in that measurement: CONTROL 06.

Performance measurement is automatic and persistent.

Key rules:
• No grace periods
• No narrative substitution
• Metrics over explanations

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.3

==================================================
NBBF 07.4. Variance Definition
==================================================

A **Variance** exists when:
• Costs exceed authorization
• Outcomes fall short of commitments
• Scope is exceeded
• Timelines are violated
• Signals contradict reported status

Variance is a system fact, not an accusation.

Key rules:
• Variance is objective
• Intent is irrelevant
• Delay is not mitigation

Authorized cost and Value: CONTROL 06.
Signal operating rules: CONTROL 00B.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.4

==================================================
NBBF 07.5. Signal-Triggered Review
==================================================

Material variance automatically triggers audit review.

Review thresholds are:
• Declared at authorization
• Consistent across similar Nodes
• Non-discretionary

This CONTROL SHALL NOT duplicate Signal reporting rights, reporting duties,
suppression rules, escalation, evidence preservation, interference,
close-out, or lifecycle.

Those operating rules: CONTROL 00B.

Key rules:
• Automatic audit review upon material variance
• No managerial veto of authorized audit review

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.5

==================================================
NBBF 07.6. Audit Scope and Access
==================================================

Audits examine:
• Node configuration
• Financial flows
• Operational execution
• Signal integrity
• Compliance with post-authorization rules, including post-vote rules
  where a vote is the applicable Decision-Node method

Audit access MAY be classified,
but accountability SHALL NOT be suspended.

Key rules:
• Full access to auditors
• Classification limits visibility, not audit
• No “need-to-know” obstruction

Classified-node Signal rules: CONTROL 00B.
Decision-Node ontology: CONTROL 01.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.6

==================================================
NBBF 07.7. Performance Tiers
==================================================

Nodes are evaluated into performance tiers, such as:
• Performing as authorized
• Underperforming
• Failing
• Abusive

Tier assignment is evidence-based
and recalculated each cycle.

Key rules:
• Tiering is public (within classification limits)
• Tiers drive consequences
• No permanent status

Publication mechanics: CONTROL 11.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.7

==================================================
NBBF 07.8. Corrective Action
==================================================

Audit corrective action MAY include:
• Configuration changes below the Decision Node
• Scope reduction
• Funding reduction
• Mandatory restructuring
• Owner replacement

Corrective action does not require scandal or exposure.

Key rules:
• Proportional response
• Structural correction
• Speed over ceremony

Operating Exit/Disengagement: CONTROL 00D.
Detailed clawback and recovery: CONTROL 08.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.8

==================================================
NBBF 07.9. Clawback and Recovery — Audit Boundary
==================================================

When abuse or gross failure is identified, CONTROL 07:

• SHALL record the audit finding
• MAY determine that authorized performance requirements were not met
• MAY trigger or recommend review under applicable recovery/clawback
  authority
• SHALL preserve evidence needed by future recovery proceedings

CONTROL 07 SHALL NOT occupy CONTROL 08's authoritative home.

DETAILED CLAWBACK / RECOVERY OPERATING MECHANICS ARE GOVERNED BY
CONTROL 08.

This CONTROL does not invent clawback formulas, repayment schedules,
recovery percentages, collection mechanisms, contractor recovery
procedure, penalties, tribunals, or enforcement offices.

Clawback is a system function, not a punishment. That principle is
preserved. Its operating mechanics are not specified here.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.9

==================================================
NBBF 07.10. Executive Accountability
==================================================

Executive owners are evaluated on:
• Performance delivery
• Signal integrity
• Responsiveness to variance
• Avoidance of trivial activation
• Respect for post-authorization constraints, including post-vote
  constraints where a vote is the applicable Decision-Node method

Repeated failure results in loss of authority.

Key rules:
• Authority is conditional
• Performance is mandatory
• Tenure is not protection

Signal integrity operating rules: CONTROL 00B.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.10

==================================================
NBBF 07.11. Audit Independence
==================================================

Audit functions operate independently of:
• Program management
• Political offices
• Budget negotiation processes

Audit interference constitutes a structural violation.

Key rules:
• Independence enforced
• Interference escalates automatically
• Retaliation prohibited

Signal non-interference operating rules: CONTROL 00B.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.11

==================================================
NBBF 07.12. Failure Is Informative
==================================================

Failure under NBBF is not hidden.

Failure:
• Generates learning
• Informs future authorization
• Reduces repeat waste
• Improves system resilience

Cover-up is worse than failure.

Key rules:
• Failure is visible
• Lessons propagate
• Silence is violation

“Informative” here means failure produces knowledge, feedback, and system
learning. It does not establish NBEF.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.12

==================================================
NBBF 07.13. Audit and Performance North Star
==================================================

Authority SHALL be earned continuously.
Performance SHALL be provable.
Failure SHALL surface.
Correction SHALL follow.

This CONTROL is binding within the audit and performance authority
delegated by CONTROL 00.

CONTROL 00 remains the system-level Root lock.

INFORMATIVE: https://BlueprintLiberty.com/NBBF/07#07.13
