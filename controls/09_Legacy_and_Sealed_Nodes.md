Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF)

AUTHORITATIVE HOME
==================================================

This CONTROL answers:

HOW ARE INHERITED, INCOMPLETE, LEGACY, OR LAWFULLY SEALED GOVERNMENTAL
NODES TREATED WITHOUT ALLOWING HISTORY, INCOMPLETENESS, OR LAWFUL SECRECY
TO BECOME HIDDEN AUTHORITY?

CONTROL 01 answers: WHAT IS A NODE AND WHAT RELATIONSHIPS CAN EXIST?
CONTROL 04 answers: HOW ARE AUTHORIZED NODES COMPOSED INTO AN OPERATING
SYSTEM?
CONTROL 00B answers: HOW DO SIGNALS OPERATE, INCLUDING CLASSIFIED-NODE
REPORTING?
CONTROL 00D answers: HOW DOES AUTHORIZED EXIT AND DISENGAGEMENT OPERATE?
CONTROL 07 answers: HOW DOES AUDIT AND PERFORMANCE OPERATE?

Legacy Node, as a permitted Node type, remains CONTROL 01.

Secret Node, as vocabulary for a Node whose details are classified for
legitimate national-security or other lawful secrecy reasons, remains
CONTROL 02.

Sealed treatment, as used in this CONTROL, is a specialized condition.
It is not a Node type.

Legacy treatment, as used in this CONTROL, is specialized handling of
inherited, incomplete, or migrating Nodes. It is not a new Node type
and SHALL NOT redefine CONTROL 01’s Legacy Node type.

Concise vocabulary: CONTROL 02.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes specialized treatment for:

• Nodes inherited from a legacy governmental structure
• Nodes whose records are incomplete
• Nodes undergoing migration toward current NBBF requirements
• Nodes subject to lawful sealing, confidentiality, security, or
  restricted-access requirements
• Nodes that are simultaneously Legacy-treated and Sealed-treated

The purpose is to represent those conditions without allowing historical
opacity, incomplete records, classification, confidentiality, or lawful
secrecy to become mechanisms for hidden authority, hidden ownership,
hidden failure, or permanent exemption from accountability.

This CONTROL does not create a second ontology, Signal system,
property-rights system, exit system, system-composition model,
republican-government model, review calendar, cost/value system, audit
system, recovery system, legislative procedure, API system, procurement
system, or education framework.

==================================================
2. FAIL-CLOSED DISTINCTIONS
==================================================

If the authority for Legacy treatment or Sealed treatment cannot be
established, this CONTROL SHALL NOT invent it.

The following distinctions SHALL be preserved:

• Unknown SHALL remain unknown.
• Unverified SHALL remain unverified.
• Restricted SHALL NOT automatically mean nonexistent.
• Incomplete SHALL NOT automatically mean invalid.
• Legacy SHALL NOT automatically mean authorized.
• Sealed SHALL NOT automatically mean lawful.

A gap MAY be recorded as a gap.

A gap SHALL NOT be filled with invented facts, placeholder owners,
inferred relationships presented as known, or software-generated
certainty.

Unauthorized action remains NULL under CONTROL 00.

==================================================
3. CONSUMPTION OF EXISTING ONTOLOGY AND VOCABULARY
==================================================

This CONTROL uses, and does not replace, CONTROL 01 and CONTROL 02.

CONTROL 01 remains authoritative for:

• What constitutes a Node
• Permitted Node types, including Legacy Node
• Human Node and Human Owner
• Decision Node, Assembly Node, Detail Node, and Support Node
• Required Node properties
• Node relationships, hierarchy, lifecycle, and ownership ontology

CONTROL 01 states that a Legacy Node represents pre-existing obligations,
that its scope is sealed and non-expansive, and that it is subject to
sunset and termination rules. This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine those
ontology facts.

CONTROL 02 remains authoritative for the term Secret Node: a Node whose
details are classified for legitimate national-security or other lawful
secrecy reasons, and which still has owners, costs, and exits.

This CONTROL MAY apply Legacy treatment or Sealed treatment to an
otherwise valid Node of a type already authorized by CONTROL 01.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT:

• Create a Sealed Node type
• Create a Secret Node type
• Convert Legacy treatment into a seventh ontology type
• Silently reclassify a Node’s CONTROL 01 type
• Treat a committee, office, process, software model, or pooled fund as
  the Human Owner

Type remains CONTROL 01.
Treatment remains this CONTROL.

==================================================
4. LEGACY TREATMENT — QUALIFICATION
==================================================

A Node MAY receive Legacy treatment only where evidence supports one or
more of the following:

• The Node is a Legacy Node under CONTROL 01
• The Node is inherited from a pre-existing governmental structure
• The Node carries a pre-existing obligation that predates current NBBF
  documentation requirements
• The Node is incompletely documented relative to current NBBF
  requirements
• The Node is undergoing documented migration toward current NBBF
  requirements

Legacy treatment MAY be requested, recorded, or identified. Recording
Legacy treatment does not by itself authorize the Node, expand its
scope, create funding, or waive other CONTROLs.

A Node that is merely old, inconvenient, politically sensitive, or poorly
managed does not qualify for Legacy treatment without qualifying evidence.

Newly created authority SHALL NOT be labeled Legacy in order to avoid
current NBBF requirements.

==================================================
5. LEGACY EVIDENCE
==================================================

Evidence establishing Legacy treatment SHOULD, to the maximum extent
available, identify:

• Why the Node is inherited, incomplete, or migrating
• What source records exist
• What source records are missing
• What facts are known
• What facts are unknown
• What facts are unverified
• The lawful or historical origin claimed for the Node, if any
• The Human Owner, if known
• The applicable Decision Node, if known

Absence of complete evidence MAY support Legacy treatment as an
incomplete inherited condition. It SHALL NOT be converted into a complete
invented history.

If no evidence can establish that Legacy treatment is warranted, this
CONTROL SHALL NOT invent Legacy status.

==================================================
6. MINIMUM IDENTITY AND UNKNOWN FACTS
==================================================

A Legacy-treated Node SHALL remain an identifiable Node to the maximum
extent permitted by available evidence and controlling lawful authority.

Minimum identity SHOULD include, where evidence exists:

• That the Node exists or is claimed to exist
• A unique identifier, or a provisional identifier expressly marked as
  provisional
• A plain-language name or a recorded unnamed/unknown-name gap
• Node type under CONTROL 01, or a recorded unknown-type gap
• Human Owner under CONTROL 01, or a recorded unknown-owner gap
• Parent or Decision-Node attribution, or a recorded unknown-relationship
  gap
• Lawful authority basis, or a recorded unknown-authority gap

This CONTROL does not define identifier formats, schemas, or databases.

Unknown ownership SHALL be recorded as unknown.

Unknown ownership SHALL NOT be filled by:

• A committee as owner
• An office as owner
• A software system as owner
• Artificial intelligence as owner
• Silence treated as an owner

CONTROL 01 remains the home for Human Owner ontology. Legacy treatment
does not waive the requirement that public authority terminate in an
identifiable human owner. An unknown-owner gap is a surfaced defect
requiring resolution. It is not permission to operate indefinitely
without a Human Owner, and it is not a hidden owner.

==================================================
7. INCOMPLETE RECORDS AND UNRESOLVED RELATIONSHIPS
==================================================

Incomplete historical records SHALL be represented as incomplete.

Unresolved relationships SHALL be represented as unresolved.

This CONTROL MAY record that a relationship is:

• Known
• Claimed but unverified
• Unknown
• Disputed
• Restricted under Sealed treatment
• Not applicable

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent a parent, child, dependency, Decision Node,
or funding relationship in order to complete a diagram.

Incomplete SHALL NOT automatically mean invalid.

Invalidity, ineligibility for funding, and ontology defects remain
governed by CONTROL 01 and other applicable CONTROLs. This CONTROL
permits an inherited incomplete Node to be represented while gaps are
known. It does not declare an incomplete Node fully compliant, and it
does not authorize new spending from incompleteness.

Unresolved gaps SHALL be surfaced. They SHALL NOT be hidden behind the
word “legacy.”

==================================================
8. MIGRATION TOWARD CURRENT NBBF REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

A Legacy-treated Node MAY be recorded as undergoing migration toward
current NBBF requirements.

Migration is a documented condition of remaining gaps and completed
repairs. It is not a new Node type.

A migration record SHOULD identify:

• Which required identities, relationships, or records remain incomplete
• Which have been completed
• That completed work does not erase prior incomplete history

Migration SHALL NOT:

• Invent missing historical facts
• Silently erase Legacy records
• Convert unknown into known without evidence
• Create new authority under color of cleanup
• Remove Legacy treatment without the evidence required by Section 9

Gaps remaining during migration SHALL remain reviewable under CONTROL 05.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent review cadence, review-due dates, or a
reauthorization calendar.

==================================================
9. REMOVAL OR EXPIRATION OF LEGACY TREATMENT
==================================================

Legacy treatment SHALL NOT be a permanent permission for opacity.

Legacy treatment MAY be removed when evidence establishes that:

• Required identity, ownership, relationship, and authority records are
  no longer incomplete relative to the applicable CONTROLs; or
• The Node has lawfully exited or been terminated under CONTROL 00D; or
• The Node has been replaced by a successor Node and lineage of the
  predecessor is preserved.

Removal of Legacy treatment SHALL be evidence-based.

Removal of Legacy treatment SHALL NOT erase the fact that the Node was
previously Legacy-treated.

Silence, administrative convenience, software migration, or passage of
time SHALL NOT by themselves remove Legacy treatment or convert unknown
facts into known facts.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a review clock for expiration. CONTROL 05
owns review cadence and reauthorization. CONTROL 00D owns operational
Exit. CONTROL 01 owns type reclassification.

==================================================
10. INHERITED AUTHORITY VERSUS NEW AUTHORITY
==================================================

Inherited authority and newly created authority SHALL remain
distinguishable.

Legacy treatment MAY describe inherited authority, pre-existing
obligation, or incomplete historical documentation.

Legacy treatment SHALL NOT:

• Create new authority
• Enlarge inherited scope
• Convert an undocumented custom into NBBF authorization
• Permit new Nodes to be born as Legacy in order to escape current
  requirements

A newly authorized Node is not a Legacy-treated Node merely because it
replaces, absorbs, or sits beside a Legacy-treated Node.

CONTROL 03 remains the home for republican-government application of
delegated authority. This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent constitutional
authority.

==================================================
11. RESTRUCTURING AND LINEAGE
==================================================

System composition and restructuring remain CONTROL 04.

When a Legacy-treated Node is restructured, grouped, migrated, succeeded,
or composed into a System Structure:

• Prior Legacy records SHALL be preserved
• Known gaps SHALL remain attributable
• Successor relationships SHALL NOT silently replace predecessor history
• Lineage SHALL remain traceable

Restructuring SHALL NOT silently erase an existing Signal, audit, review,
recovery, Exit, funding, Decision-Node, or accountability record.

Authority is traceable or it does not exist. CONTROL 00 remains that
lock. This CONTROL applies it to Legacy lineage.

==================================================
12. SEALED TREATMENT — QUALIFICATION
==================================================

A Node, relationship, or record MAY receive Sealed treatment only where
a lawful restriction exists, such as classification, confidentiality,
security, or restricted access created by constitution, statute, charter,
ordinance, court order, or other controlling lawful authority.

This CONTROL represents lawful restrictions. It SHALL NOT manufacture the
authority for those restrictions.

Sealed treatment MAY apply to a Secret Node as defined by CONTROL 02, or
to other authorized Nodes whose information is lawfully restricted.

Sealed treatment is a condition. It is not a Node type.

If the lawful basis for Sealed treatment cannot be identified, this
CONTROL SHALL NOT invent Sealed status.

Sealed SHALL NOT automatically mean lawful.

A desire for administrative privacy, embarrassment avoidance, political
convenience, or concealment of failure does not qualify a Node for Sealed
treatment.

==================================================
13. EXISTENCE VERSUS DISCLOSURE
==================================================

The existence of restricted information SHALL be distinguished from
disclosure of the restricted information itself.

Restricted SHALL NOT automatically mean nonexistent.

Where controlling lawful authority and applicable NBBF CONTROLs permit,
a Sealed-treated Node SHOULD retain minimum public existence identity
such as that a Node exists, that Sealed treatment applies, and that
accountability continues.

CONTROL 00D already requires that classified or sealed Nodes remain
subject to exit controls and, at minimum, disclose existence, duration,
termination conditions, and renewal authority. This CONTROL SHALL NOT
create a second Exit-disclosure procedure. It requires Sealed treatment
to remain compatible with that CONTROL 00D minimum.

CONTROL 00B already requires classified-node declarations and prohibits
permanent secrecy. This CONTROL SHALL NOT duplicate those Signal
mechanics. It requires Sealed treatment to remain compatible with
CONTROL 00B.

Where law forbids public disclosure of a minimum-identity element, that
element remains restricted. Restriction is not disappearance from
structural accountability.

==================================================
14. ACCOUNTABILITY UNDER SEAL
==================================================

Sealed treatment SHALL NOT mean unaccountable.

Human Owner and Decision-Node accountability SHALL be preserved to the
maximum extent authorized by controlling lawful authority and CONTROL 01.

Where law restricts public disclosure of a Human Owner’s identity, a
lawful accountability channel SHALL remain. CONTROL 00B owns classified
reporting channels and classified-node Signal operating rules. This
CONTROL SHALL NOT invent those channels.

Sealed treatment SHALL NOT:

• Create anonymous ownership
• Replace a Human Owner with a committee, office, or machine
• Hide Decision-Node attribution where attribution itself is not
  lawfully restricted
• Convert restricted content into an absence of the Node

Costs, exits, and owners remain required in CONTROL 02’s Secret Node
vocabulary and in CONTROL 01 ontology. This CONTROL does not waive them.

==================================================
15. LAWFUL ACCESS VERSUS PUBLIC ACCESS
==================================================

Lawful access and public access are not the same.

Sealed treatment MAY restrict public visibility.

Sealed treatment SHALL NOT, by itself, deny lawful access required by
audit, oversight, or other controlling lawful authority.

CONTROL 07 already provides that audit access MAY be classified, but
accountability SHALL NOT be suspended, and classification limits
visibility, not audit. This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine that audit
doctrine.

CONTROL 00B already provides protected reporting channels for classified
or restricted nodes. This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine those channels.

==================================================
16. SEALED RECORDS SHALL NOT DISAPPEAR
==================================================

A sealed record SHALL NOT silently disappear.

Sealed treatment MAY restrict who may see a record. It SHALL NOT erase
the record, convert it into a non-record, or treat close, migration, or
software transformation as destruction of history.

CONTROL 00B owns Signal close-out and the principle that close does not
mean erase. This CONTROL SHALL NOT duplicate that Signal doctrine. It
requires that Sealed treatment not be used to evade it.

Unavailability to the public is not nonexistence.

==================================================
17. UNSEALING
==================================================

Later unsealing interacts with the Node as a change of restriction, not
as creation of a new Node and not as creation of new authority.

Unsealing MAY make previously restricted information visible to a broader
lawful audience.

Unsealing SHALL NOT:

• Erase the history that the information was sealed
• Invent facts that were unknown while sealed
• Cure incomplete Legacy evidence
• Reauthorize the Node
• Replace CONTROL 05 review or CONTROL 10 Decision-Node action

CONTROL 00B and CONTROL 05 / CONTROL 10 remain the homes for classified
visibility after an authorized secrecy period and for reauthorization of
continued secrecy. This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent that calendar or that
interface.

==================================================
18. SIGNAL, REVIEW, AUDIT, RECOVERY, AND EXIT INTERFACES
==================================================

Legacy treatment and Sealed treatment MAY identify structural interfaces
to other CONTROLs. They SHALL NOT absorb those CONTROLs.

Signals:
CONTROL 00B owns Signal lifecycle, suppression, escalation, evidence,
interference, access, unmasking, and close-out.
A Legacy-treated or Sealed-treated Node SHALL remain structurally able to
have Signals associated with it.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine reporting rights or Signal operations.
A Signal SHALL NOT be erased because the Node is Legacy-treated or
Sealed-treated.

Review:
CONTROL 05 owns review cadence, due dates, and reauthorization.
Legacy treatment and Sealed treatment SHALL NOT create a second clock.
They SHALL NOT confer permanent exemption from review.

Audit:
CONTROL 07 owns audit procedure and performance findings.
A Legacy-treated or Sealed-treated Node SHALL remain structurally able to
be audited.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine audit methods, variance, or performance
tiers.

Recovery:
CONTROL 08 owns recovery and clawback operating mechanics.
Legacy treatment and Sealed treatment SHALL NOT confer permanent
exemption from recovery.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT create recovery formulas, collection procedures,
or contractor-recovery systems.

Exit:
CONTROL 00D owns operational Exit and Disengagement.
Legacy treatment and Sealed treatment SHALL NOT destroy Exit rights.
Secrecy limits visibility, not exit, as already stated by CONTROL 00D.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a second Exit procedure.

Cost and Value:
CONTROL 06 remains the home for Cost and Value. This CONTROL SHALL NOT
redefine them.

==================================================
19. LEGACY AND SEALED COMBINATION
==================================================

A Node MAY be both Legacy-treated and Sealed-treated.

The two conditions SHALL remain distinguishable.

Legacy status SHALL NOT justify sealing.

Sealed status SHALL NOT cure incomplete Legacy evidence.

One condition SHALL NOT silently authorize the other.

Where both apply:

• Unknown facts remain unknown
• Restricted facts remain restricted, not nonexistent
• Incomplete facts remain incomplete, not invented
• Accountability remains required to the maximum extent authorized
• Lineage of both conditions remains preserved

A Sealed incomplete Legacy-treated Node is the highest-risk opacity
condition this CONTROL recognizes. It requires stricter preservation of
distinctions, not fewer.

==================================================
20. ANTI-OPACITY
==================================================

Legacy treatment and Sealed treatment SHALL NOT become structural
mechanisms for:

• Hidden authority
• Anonymous ownership
• Pooled responsibility
• Erased Signals
• Erased audit history
• Erased funding lineage
• Erased Decision-Node attribution
• Permanent exemption from review
• Permanent exemption from recovery
• Destruction of Exit rights
• Silent destruction of historical records

Where another CONTROL owns the operating consequence, this CONTROL
points to that CONTROL rather than reproducing its doctrine.

Property and pooling: CONTROL 00C.
System composition and anti-big-pool structure: CONTROL 04.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT duplicate those homes. It forbids using Legacy or
Sealed treatment to accomplish by condition what those CONTROLs forbid
by structure or property right.

==================================================
21. DIGITAL REPRESENTATION
==================================================

CONTROL 11 — Public Transparency API and Publishing remains RESERVED
and empty.

This CONTROL is implementation-neutral.

Software MAY later represent Legacy treatment, Sealed treatment, known
gaps, unknown facts, restricted existence, and lineage.

The following distinctions SHALL survive any future representation:

• Known / unknown / unverified
• Restricted / nonexistent
• Incomplete / invalid
• Legacy-treated / authorized
• Sealed-treated / lawful
• Existence of a restriction / content of the restricted record
• Inherited authority / newly created authority

This CONTROL SHALL NOT define APIs, endpoints, JSON, schemas, databases,

encryption algorithms, UI behavior, or software architecture.

Software SHALL NOT create the underlying authority for Legacy treatment
or Sealed treatment.

==================================================
22. HUMAN AUTHORITY AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
==================================================

No digital Legacy or Sealed model SHALL replace human or constitutional
authority.

Artificial intelligence MAY analyze available evidence and flag apparent
inconsistencies, missing evidence, or incompatible conditions.

Artificial intelligence SHALL NOT independently:

• Create a Node
• Remove a Node
• Apply or remove Legacy treatment
• Apply, remove, seal, or unseal Sealed treatment
• Authorize, reauthorize, or reclassify a Node
• Fill unknown facts

Human accountability remains governed by CONTROL 00 and CONTROL 01.

==================================================
25. ENFORCEMENT
==================================================

This CONTROL owns specialized Legacy and Sealed treatment rules.

Noncompliance includes:

• Using Legacy treatment to hide authority, ownership, failure, or
  incomplete records
• Using Sealed treatment to mean unaccountable or nonexistent
• Inventing unknown facts
• Treating incomplete as automatically invalid, or complete without
  evidence
• Treating Legacy as automatically authorized
• Treating Sealed as automatically lawful
• Using Legacy status to justify sealing
• Using Sealed status to cure incomplete Legacy evidence
• Erasing lineage through migration, restructuring, sealing, or unsealing
• Treating this CONTROL as ontology, Signal operations, Exit, review
  calendar, audit, recovery, system composition, procurement, API
  mechanics, NBEF, or Root authority
• Allowing software or artificial intelligence to create, seal, unseal,
  or reclassify a Node

Detection MAY occur through:

• Authorized NBBF CONTROL stewardship under CONTROL 00
• Signals under CONTROL 00B
• Review under CONTROL 05
• Audit under CONTROL 07

Detailed audit procedure: CONTROL 07.
This CONTROL does not create an enforcement agency, tribunal, board,
criminal penalty, civil penalty, or new governmental power.

28. NORTH STAR
==================================================

History may be incomplete.
Secrecy may be lawful.
Neither may become a hiding place.

Unknown stays unknown.
Restricted stays restricted, not gone.
Legacy stays a condition, not a pardon.
Seal stays a limit on sight, not a limit on answerability.

This CONTROL is binding within the Legacy and Sealed Node treatment
authority delegated by CONTROL 00.

CONTROL 00 remains the system-level Root lock.
