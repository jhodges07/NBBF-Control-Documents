==================================================

REPRESENTED, AND MADE MACHINE-ACCESSIBLE SO THAT CITIZENS CAN
INDEPENDENTLY INSPECT THE OPERATION OF THE NBBF SYSTEM WITHOUT THIS
CONTROL BECOMING A SECOND HOME FOR THE UNDERLYING GOVERNMENTAL

CONTROL 00 answers: WHAT IS NBBF ROOT AUTHORITY, INCLUDING PUBLICATION

CONTROL 03 answers: HOW DOES NBBF APPLY REPUBLICAN GOVERNMENT PRINCIPLES,

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes the public transparency, publication, and
machine-accessible representation rules of NBBF.

Its purpose is to ensure that citizens and other lawful observers can
independently inspect published NBBF operation — Nodes, ownership,
authority relationships, funding movement, Decisions, Signals, review,
audit, recovery, Exit, lineage, and Legacy/Sealed conditions — without
this CONTROL becoming the source of those facts or a new opacity layer.

This CONTROL does not create a second ontology, Signal system,
property-rights system, exit system, republican-government model,
system-composition model, review calendar, cost/value system, audit
system, recovery system, Legacy/Sealed treatment system, legislative
procedure, procurement system, or education framework.

==================================================
2. PUBLICATION IS REPRESENTATION, NOT AUTHORITY
==================================================

Publishing a Node, Signal, expenditure, relationship, decision, review
state, audit fact, recovery fact, Exit fact, Legacy or Sealed condition,
or other record does not create, enlarge, validate, cure, renew, or
extinguish the underlying authority.

The authoritative source remains its governing CONTROL and lawful
human or governmental action.

Published ≠ authorized.
Unpublished ≠ unauthorized.

A missing, delayed, incomplete, or incorrect publication SHALL NOT be
treated as the legal condition of the underlying Node or act.

Correction of a publication SHALL NOT itself reauthorize, terminate, or
amend the underlying authority.

==================================================
3. API AND LEDGER AS ARCHITECTURAL CAPABILITIES
==================================================

API, as used in this CONTROL, means a public machine-accessible interface
capability through which independent systems MAY retrieve published NBBF
information.

API does not mean REST, GraphQL, a particular payload format, a vendor
product, a programming language, or a software stack.

Ledger, as used in this CONTROL, means the structural requirement that
published public history remain retrievable and that later publication
not silently erase prior public states.

Ledger does not mean a blockchain, distributed-ledger product, token,
consensus protocol, or specific database.

This CONTROL v1.0.2 is implementation-neutral.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT mandate:

• REST
• GraphQL
• JSON
• XML
• SQL
• a specific database
• a specific cloud platform
• blockchain
• a particular programming language
• a vendor product
• a specific authentication product
• a specific encryption product
• a specific user-interface framework
• endpoint paths
• schemas
• payload layouts
• hosting topology
• dashboard or mobile-application design

A later implementation MAY choose technologies. Those choices are not
this CONTROL and SHALL NOT become this CONTROL by use.

==================================================
4. PUBLIC BY DEFAULT WITH LAWFUL RESTRICTIONS
==================================================

NBBF operational information SHALL be publicly inspectable to the
maximum extent lawfully permitted.

Transparency is the default.
Secrecy requires legitimate authority and justification.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent secrecy law.

CONTROL 09 remains authoritative for Legacy and Sealed treatment.
CONTROL 00B remains authoritative for classified-node Signal operating
rules.
CONTROL 02 remains authoritative for the term Secret Node.

Restricted content SHALL NOT be treated as nonexistent merely because
the content cannot lawfully be disclosed.

Where lawful, the system SHOULD preserve the observable fact that a
restricted record, relationship, Signal, or condition exists without
publishing protected content.

Existence of a restriction ≠ disclosure of restricted content.
Restricted ≠ nonexistent.
Sealed ≠ lawful merely because it is marked sealed.

“Security,” “legacy,” “sealed,” “technical limitation,” or “system
migration” SHALL NOT be used as invented authority to hide otherwise
publishable information.

==================================================
5. MACHINE ACCESSIBILITY
==================================================

Public information SHALL be capable of machine access so independent
systems can retrieve and analyze published NBBF information.

Machine accessibility is a capability requirement. It is not a protocol
mandate.

Machine access that is technically declared but practically unusable —
through undocumented proprietary dependencies, undisclosed formats, or
artificial obstruction — does not satisfy this CONTROL.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT require a citizen or independent observer to
obtain a proprietary vendor license as a condition of inspecting
lawfully public NBBF information.

Independent retrieval and analysis do not create NBBF authority.

==================================================
6. HUMAN READABILITY
==================================================

Machine accessibility SHALL NOT replace citizen readability.

Published information SHALL remain capable of being presented in a form
ordinary citizens can inspect and understand.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT require citizens to become programmers merely to
observe public governmental operation.

A machine-accessible representation MAY exist alongside a human-readable
representation. Neither MAY be used to withhold required public facts
that the other would otherwise expose.

Citizen telemetry, as an observational principle, remains CONTROL 03 and
CONTROL 10 as those CONTROLs already state it. This CONTROL supplies
publication mechanics for that observation. It is not surveillance of
private citizens.

==================================================
7. TRACEABILITY
==================================================

Published representations SHALL preserve enough identity and lineage to
trace authorized relationships across the NBBF system without inventing
missing facts.

Where applicable and lawfully public, representation SHOULD permit
tracing of:

• Node identity
• Responsible Human Owner
• Authority relationship
• Funding movement
• Decision-Node relationship
• Signal attachment
• Review status
• Audit status
• Recovery status
• Exit status
• Predecessor/successor lineage
• Legacy/Sealed treatment

This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine those concepts.

Node identity and Human Owner: CONTROL 01.
Authority and republican application: CONTROL 00 and CONTROL 03.
Funding as property/pooling: CONTROL 00C.
Cost and Value: CONTROL 06.
System composition: CONTROL 04.
Signals: CONTROL 00B.
Review: CONTROL 05.
Audit: CONTROL 07.
Recovery: CONTROL 08.
Exit: CONTROL 00D.
Decision-Node interface: CONTROL 10.
Legacy/Sealed treatment: CONTROL 09.

Aggregation SHALL NOT be used to break that traceability or to hide
responsible Nodes or Human Owners behind organizational labels.

==================================================
8. UNKNOWN, UNVERIFIED, AND RESTRICTED SEMANTICS
==================================================

Publication SHALL preserve these distinctions:

• Unknown ≠ zero
• Unknown ≠ nonexistent
• Unverified ≠ verified
• Restricted ≠ nonexistent
• Incomplete ≠ complete
• Published ≠ authorized
• Unpublished ≠ unauthorized
• Sealed ≠ lawful merely because it is marked sealed

This CONTROL SHALL NOT manufacture facts to make a publication appear
complete.

Unknown values SHALL NOT be converted into zeros, empty completeness,
or implied current authorization.

Where a fact is unknown, unverified, incomplete, restricted, stale, or
unavailable, the published representation SHALL keep that condition
distinguishable.

CONTROL 09 remains the home for unknown/unverified/restricted treatment
of Legacy and Sealed Nodes. This CONTROL SHALL NOT occupy that home. It
requires publication to preserve those distinctions when representing
them.

==================================================
9. HISTORY, LINEAGE, AND CORRECTION
==================================================

Publication updates SHALL NOT silently erase prior public history.

Corrections, migrations, restructuring, sealing, unsealing, replacement,
or later publication SHALL preserve appropriate lineage.

A material correction SHALL remain distinguishable from the prior
published state.

Corrections SHALL NOT silently rewrite history.

This CONTROL defines the required structural result: public history
remains retrievable and prior states remain distinguishable. It does
not invent an immutable-ledger implementation, blockchain, or specific
storage technology.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine CONTROL 00 publication, immutability, or
versioning of CONTROL documents.

Successor publication of a Node, system, or record SHALL NOT silently
replace predecessor history.

==================================================
10. SIGNAL NON-INTERFERENCE
==================================================

CONTROL 00B remains authoritative for Signal lifecycle, suppression,
escalation, evidence, interference, access, unmasking, and close-out.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT become a mechanism for hiding, suppressing,
rewriting, delaying, or destroying Signals.

Publication mechanics SHALL preserve the ability to represent Signal
existence and status according to lawful access boundaries.

A Signal SHALL NOT be erased from public representation merely because
a publication system was migrated, summarized, aggregated, or sealed
without lawful Sealed treatment under CONTROL 09 and Signal rules under
CONTROL 00B.

Close of a Signal remains CONTROL 00B. Close does not mean erase.
Publication of close SHALL NOT mean erasure of public history of that
Signal where the Signal was lawfully public.

==================================================
11. HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY
==================================================

Software, artificial intelligence, databases, APIs, publication
services, contractors, committees, or automated systems SHALL NOT
become substitutes for the Human Owner requirement established by
CONTROL 00 and CONTROL 01.

This CONTROL MAY expose accountability.
It does not create substitute ownership.

A publication custodian, implementer, or operator is not the Human
Owner of the published Node unless that person is already the Human
Owner under CONTROL 01.

==================================================
12. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
==================================================

Artificial intelligence MAY assist with:

• Indexing
• Search
• Summarization
• Anomaly detection
• Inconsistency detection
• Accessibility
• Presentation

Artificial intelligence SHALL NOT silently:

• Create authority
• Alter authoritative facts
• Fill unknown facts
• Fabricate lineage
• Suppress material information
• Seal or unseal records
• Declare compliance
• Replace required human authority

Where material, AI-generated interpretation SHALL remain distinguishable
from the underlying authoritative or public record.

A summary produced by software or AI SHALL NOT replace required
underlying public facts.

==================================================
13. ANTI-OPACITY
==================================================

Publication architecture SHALL NOT be used to create a new opacity layer.

The following are prohibited as publication practices:

• Publishing summaries while withholding required underlying public facts
• Breaking traceability through aggregation
• Erasing predecessor history
• Hiding responsibility behind organizational labels
• Converting unknown values into zeros
• Suppressing material variance
• Obscuring funding pathways
• Concealing Node relationships
• Making machine access technically available but practically unusable
• Creating undocumented proprietary dependencies that prevent lawful
  public inspection
• Using interface design to materially misrepresent underlying facts
• Using “security,” “legacy,” “sealed,” “technical limitation,” or
  “system migration” as invented authority to hide otherwise publishable
  information

Variance, performance, and audit facts remain CONTROL 07.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine them. It SHALL NOT hide them through
publication design.

==================================================
14. AVAILABILITY AND FAILURE BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Publication-system behavior SHALL be fail-visible.

A publication-system failure SHALL NOT silently transform unavailable
information into apparently valid zero, empty, complete, or current
information.

Where publication is unavailable, stale, incomplete, delayed, or
unverified, that condition SHALL remain distinguishable.

Stale ≠ current.
Unavailable ≠ empty.
Delayed ≠ complete.

This CONTROL does not create uptime percentages, service-level numbers,
retry intervals, or technical monitoring protocols.

Root fail-closed behavior remains CONTROL 00: if Root is missing,
invalid, unverifiable, or corrupted, no CONTROLs publish. This CONTROL
does not displace that lock.

==================================================
15. PRIVACY AND LAWFUL DISCLOSURE
==================================================

Transparency does not authorize disclosure of information that lawfully
must remain protected.

This CONTROL SHALL distinguish public accountability from unauthorized
personal or restricted disclosure.

Citizen telemetry is observation of governmental operation. It is not
authority to collect, publish, or traffic in protected personal data.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent privacy statutes, classification rules,
retention periods, access classes, or legal exceptions.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a broad personal-data collection authority
merely to support transparency.

Where law requires protection, publication SHALL restrict content and
SHALL NOT convert the restricted record into a non-record.

17. DIGITAL TWIN COMPATIBILITY
==================================================

Governmental structures and activities MAY later be represented through
a Digital Twin of the Republic or related runtime representation.

CONTROL 00 identifies CDT and UNBKE as independent implementations that
SHALL NOT replace human accountability.

This CONTROL SHOULD permit later representation through such a twin
without making those implementation mechanisms normative.

A later digital representation SHALL preserve, at minimum:

• Publication is representation, not authority
• Public-by-default with lawful restrictions
• Restricted ≠ nonexistent
• Unknown / unverified / incomplete distinctions
• Human readability alongside machine accessibility
• Traceability of lawfully public relationships
• Non-erasure of public lineage
• Signal non-interference
• Fail-visible unavailability
• Human Owner accountability
• Distinguishability of AI interpretation from underlying records

This CONTROL SHALL NOT design dashboard screens, mobile applications,
databases, API endpoints, endpoint paths, schemas, JSON payloads,
blockchain architecture, authentication workflows, hosting topology, or
user-interface layouts.

CDT, UNBKE, and related systems remain independent. They MAY represent
published NBBF information. They SHALL NOT become this CONTROL.

==================================================

This CONTROL owns public transparency representation and publication
rules.

Noncompliance includes:

• Treating publication as creation, cure, or extinction of authority
• Treating unpublished as unauthorized, or published as authorized
• Converting unknown, restricted, stale, or unavailable information into
  apparent zeros, emptiness, completeness, or currency
• Using publication to hide, suppress, rewrite, delay, or destroy
  Signals
• Using summaries, aggregation, interface design, proprietary lock-in,
  or invented “security/legacy/sealed/migration” claims to withhold
  otherwise publishable facts
• Silently erasing public lineage
• Substituting software, AI, contractors, or committees for Human Owner
• Treating this CONTROL as Signal operations, ontology, Exit, review
  calendar, audit, recovery, composition, Legacy/Sealed treatment,
  Decision-Node interface, procurement, NBEF, or Root CONTROL-document
  publication governance
• Mandating a particular technology stack as if it were this CONTROL

Detection MAY occur through:

• Authorized NBBF CONTROL stewardship under CONTROL 00
• Signals under CONTROL 00B
• Review under CONTROL 05
• Audit under CONTROL 07

Detailed audit procedure: CONTROL 07.
This CONTROL does not create an enforcement agency, tribunal, board,
criminal penalty, civil penalty, or new governmental power.
