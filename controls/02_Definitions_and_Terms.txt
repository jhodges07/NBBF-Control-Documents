1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document defines concise official meanings of terms used throughout
the Node-Based Budget Framework.

These definitions are binding for NBBF interpretation within the authority
delegated by CONTROL 00.

No NBBF term defined here may be reinterpreted, expanded, or narrowed
inside NBBF contrary to this CONTROL, except by versioned replacement
under CONTROL 00.

Where ambiguity exists among NBBF uses of a term defined in this CONTROL,
the most restrictive interpretation governing authority, spending, scope,
duration, and secrecy SHALL prevail, provided that rule does not override
CONTROL 00 or lawful governmental authority.

==================================================
2. CORE STRUCTURAL TERMS
==================================================

CONTROL 01 owns full Node ontology, including classification, relationships,
lifecycle, ownership, structural behavior, and ontology mechanics.

This CONTROL retains only concise identifying definitions.

--------------------------------------------------

2.1 Node

A **Node** is the smallest indivisible unit of budget authority,
obligation, or execution within NBBF.

A Node has a single accountable human owner, a declared purpose, and
bounded duration.

Authoritative Node classification, relationships, lifecycle, ownership,
and ontology mechanics: CONTROL 01.

--------------------------------------------------

2.2 Human Owner

A **Human Owner** is a named individual responsible for a Node.

Authoritative ownership, accountability, and removal mechanics: CONTROL 01.
Executor and guardian principles: CONTROL 00.

--------------------------------------------------

2.3 Decision Node (Voting Node)

A **Decision Node** is a Node that requires affirmative authorization by
the applicable legislative or other constitutionally designated
Decision-Node authority of that jurisdiction.

A Decision Node establishes scope, purpose, duration, and funding ceiling
for the authority it grants.

Congress is an example of a Decision-Node authority in the United States
federal legislative jurisdiction. Congress is not the universal or exclusive
NBBF Decision Node.

Authoritative Decision-Node ontology: CONTROL 01.
Jurisdiction-specific application: CONTROL 03. CONTROL 03 remains reserved
and is not drafted here.

--------------------------------------------------

2.4 Assembly Node

An **Assembly Node** decomposes a Decision Node into executable structure.

Assembly Nodes are not themselves the Decision-Node authorization.
They exist to expose cost, obligations, and configuration.

Authoritative classification and structural behavior: CONTROL 01.

--------------------------------------------------

2.5 Detail Node

A **Detail Node** represents a reusable operational, logistical,
or support element.

Detail Nodes do not pool authority or funding. Pooling operating doctrine:
CONTROL 00C.

Authoritative classification and structural behavior: CONTROL 01.

--------------------------------------------------

2.6 Support Node

A **Support Node** provides shared services or capabilities to one or more
operational Nodes.

Authoritative classification, funding references, and collapse behavior:
CONTROL 01.
Exit of consuming Nodes: CONTROL 00D.

==================================================
3. FINANCIAL AUTHORITY TERMS
==================================================

3.1 Scope

**Scope** defines the bounded purpose, actions, geography, and operational
limits of a Node.

Actions outside declared scope are unauthorized.

--------------------------------------------------

3.2 Funding Ceiling

A **Funding Ceiling** is the maximum authorized allocation for a Node
within its authorized cycle.

Ceilings SHALL NOT be exceeded, replenished, or rolled forward without
reauthorization.

--------------------------------------------------

3.3 Four-Month Budget Cycle

The **Four-Month Budget Cycle** is the existing NBBF vocabulary term for
the standard authorization, defense, and review period.

No funding persists beyond the cycle without affirmative renewal.

Review-calendar mechanics: CONTROL 05. CONTROL 05 remains reserved and is
not drafted here.

--------------------------------------------------

3.4 Reprogramming

**Reprogramming** is the transfer or redirection of funds after
Decision-Node authorization.

Under NBBF:
• Reprogramming is permitted only below a Decision Node
• Cross–Decision-Node reprogramming requires re-authorization by the
  applicable Decision-Node authority
• Unauthorized reprogramming is void ab initio

Federal legislative example: a Congressional re-vote may be the applicable
Decision-Node re-authorization. That example does not define Decision-Node
authority for every jurisdiction.

==================================================
4. SINKING FUND AND RESERVE TERMS
==================================================

These terms are preserved NBBF vocabulary. Cost and value mechanics:
CONTROL 06. This CONTROL does not absorb CONTROL 06.

--------------------------------------------------

4.1 Sinking Fund Node

A **Sinking Fund Node** is a pre-authorized Decision Node established to
enable immediate executive action under defined crisis or defense conditions.

A Sinking Fund Node has explicit activation triggers, a hard funding
ceiling, and no automatic replenishment.

Ontology and decomposition requirements: CONTROL 01.
Cost treatment: CONTROL 06.

--------------------------------------------------

4.2 Activation Event

An **Activation Event** occurs when funds are drawn from a Sinking Fund
Node or Reserve Node.

--------------------------------------------------

4.3 Parked Balance (Debt-Offset State)

A **Parked Balance** is an unused portion of a Sinking Fund Node placed
in a non-spendable state for debt-offset purposes.

Where a national treasury and national debt exist, parked balances may be
described as temporarily loaned for retiring that debt. That description is
an application example, not a universal jurisdictional requirement.

Parked balances are not available for activation while parked, do not
constitute spending, and SHALL NOT be leveraged, traded, or pledged.

Cost and value mechanics: CONTROL 06.

--------------------------------------------------

4.4 Interest Savings Credit

An **Interest Savings Credit** is the quantified reduction in interest
expense resulting from parked balances.

Interest-savings allocation mechanics: CONTROL 06.

==================================================
5. SIGNALING AND ACCOUNTABILITY TERMS
==================================================

5.1 Signal

A **Signal** is any observable indication of performance, delivery,
failure, or variance generated by or concerning a Node.

This CONTROL owns that concise vocabulary definition.

CONTROL 00B owns Signal operating rules, including reporting rights,
reporting duties, suppression rules, escalation, evidence requirements,
close-out, interference consequences, Signal lifecycle, and Signal review.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT independently establish those operating rules.

--------------------------------------------------

5.2 Reporting Surface

A **Reporting Surface** is the defined interface through which Signals
are submitted, received, or persisted.

Signal operating rules for reporting surfaces: CONTROL 00B.
Node reporting-surface requirements: CONTROL 01.

--------------------------------------------------

5.3 Exit Condition

An **Exit Condition** is a predefined trigger that terminates, suspends,
or modifies a Node.

Exit conditions may be time-based, performance-based, audit-based, or
authority-based.

Operating exit and disengagement: CONTROL 00D.
Node ontology exit fields: CONTROL 01.
Recovery and clawback mechanics: CONTROL 08. CONTROL 08 remains reserved
and is not drafted here.

==================================================
6. SECRECY AND WAR-AUTHORITY TERMS
==================================================

6.1 Secret Node

A **Secret Node** is a Node whose details are classified for legitimate
national-security or other lawful secrecy reasons.

Secret Nodes still have owners, costs, and exits.

Classified-node reporting and visibility operating rules: CONTROL 00B.
Ontology: CONTROL 01.

--------------------------------------------------

6.2 Declaration of War (Functional)

A **Declaration of War (Functional)** occurs when the applicable
Decision-Node authority authorizes and funds a Decision Node whose scope
includes the use of military force.

Federal legislative example: Congress authorizing and funding such a
Decision Node. That example does not define war-authorization structure
for every jurisdiction.

Jurisdiction-specific application: CONTROL 03. CONTROL 03 remains reserved
and is not drafted here.
