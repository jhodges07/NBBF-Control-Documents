AUTHORITATIVE HOME
==================================================

This CONTROL answers:

HOW DOES NBBF APPLY REPUBLICAN GOVERNMENT PRINCIPLES TO TAXPAYER-FUNDED
GOVERNMENTAL JURISDICTIONS?

CONTROL 00 answers:

WHAT IS NBBF ROOT AUTHORITY?

CONTROL 01 answers:

WHAT IS A NODE?

This CONTROL applies Node classes, Human Owners, and Decision Nodes declared
by CONTROL 01. It SHALL NOT redefine them.

Concise vocabulary: CONTROL 02.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes the Republican Government Model of NBBF.

Its purpose is to ensure that taxpayer-funded governmental activity operates
through delegated authority and remains accountable to the people from whom
that authority originates.

This CONTROL defines the structural relationship among:

• Citizens
• Taxpayers
• Human Owners
• Governmental jurisdictions
• Representative institutions
• Decision Nodes
• Governmental Nodes
• Delegated authority
• Public accountability
• Taxpayer-funded activity
• Republican consent and representation

This CONTROL does not create a second ontology, Signal system, property-rights
system, exit system, cost/value system, audit system, procurement system,
API system, or education framework.

==================================================
2. GOVERNMENT AS DELEGATED AUTHORITY
==================================================

Governmental authority is delegated authority.

Government SHALL NOT be treated as an independent ownership authority over
the people, the jurisdiction, or taxpayer resources.

Elected and lawfully appointed representatives exercise delegated authority.
They do not become the owners of the governmental system.

A governmental Node administers or executes authorized public purpose. It
does not acquire proprietary ownership of the republic by occupying office,
holding a budget, or operating a process.

Where property, pooling, or stewardship of resources is at issue, operating
doctrine is CONTROL 00C. This CONTROL does not restate that doctrine.

==================================================
3. CITIZEN AND TAXPAYER ACCOUNTABILITY
==================================================

Taxpayer supremacy and citizen supremacy, as used in this CONTROL, are
accountability principles.

They mean that governmental authority remains answerable to the people of
the jurisdiction from which that authority is delegated, and that
taxpayer-funded activity remains capable of public accounting.

They do not invent a new governmental power, office, tribunal, or property
right.

They do not override constitution, statute, charter, ordinance, or other
controlling lawful authority.

They do not convert CONTROL 00 Pillar 1 into a second property-rights
CONTROL. Property and pooling: CONTROL 00C.

==================================================
4. HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY
==================================================

Every governmental Node ultimately SHALL remain traceable to accountable
human authority consistent with CONTROL 01.

Human Owner, Human Node, Executor, and Guardian meanings used here are
those established by CONTROL 00 and CONTROL 01. Concise vocabulary:
CONTROL 02.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine those terms.

Committees, agencies, automation, digital twins, and other institutions MAY
assist. They SHALL NOT replace identifiable human accountability.

==================================================
5. JURISDICTIONAL RESPONSIBILITY
==================================================

Governmental activity under NBBF SHALL have an identifiable jurisdictional
and authority basis.

A jurisdiction, for this CONTROL, is a lawful republican governing body
or taxpayer-funded public authority with a defined people, territory or
membership, and delegated public purpose.

NBBF is jurisdiction-neutral. The same republican-application principles
apply at every lawful republican level of government.

INFORMATIVE examples of jurisdictions include the United States, a State
such as Kansas, a county, a municipality, a township, a school district,
a special district, a commonwealth, a territory, and other taxpayer-funded
republican governing bodies. These examples do not make this CONTROL
Kansas-specific, federal-specific, or exclusive to any named jurisdiction.

Kansas, or any other named place, MAY appear in later implementation or
application material. Such material is not this CONTROL and SHALL NOT
narrow this CONTROL.

School districts appear here only as examples of governmental jurisdictions.
They do not establish NBEF.

==================================================
6. DECISION ACCOUNTABILITY
==================================================

Material governmental decisions SHALL be attributable to identifiable
Decision Nodes and responsible human actors.

Decision Node meaning: CONTROL 01 ontology and CONTROL 02 vocabulary.
This CONTROL applies that meaning to republican jurisdictions. It SHALL
NOT create a second Decision-Node definition.

Congress is an example of a Decision-Node authority in the United States
federal legislative jurisdiction. Congress is not the universal or exclusive
NBBF Decision Node.

The applicable Decision-Node authority of a jurisdiction is the legislative
or other constitutionally designated authorizing body of that jurisdiction.

Legislative Decision-Node interface mechanics: CONTROL 10.

Review-calendar and reauthorization-cycle mechanics: CONTROL 05.

==================================================
7. REPRESENTATION
==================================================

Republican representation is the lawful exercise of delegated authority
on behalf of the people of a jurisdiction.

Representatives SHALL remain identifiable as human actors exercising
delegated authority.

Representation SHALL NOT be treated as private ownership of government,
of Nodes, or of taxpayer resources.

Local representative institutions retain legitimate decision authority
within their jurisdiction, subject to higher lawful authority and to the
accountability requirements of this CONTROL and CONTROL 00.

==================================================
8. TAXPAYER-FUNDED ACTIVITY
==================================================

Collection, allocation, commitment, expenditure, performance, and
disposition of taxpayer resources SHALL be capable of accountability under
the appropriate CONTROL homes.

This CONTROL requires that capability. It does not absorb those homes.

• Property, pooling, and stewardship of granted resources: CONTROL 00C
• Cost and value measurement: CONTROL 06
• Audit, performance, and variance: CONTROL 07
• Signals reporting operational reality: CONTROL 00B
• Exit and disengagement: CONTROL 00D
• Recovery and clawback operating mechanics: CONTROL 08
• Competitive Node Procurement: CONTROL 12

This CONTROL SHALL NOT specify bidding systems, prime or subcontractor
structures, contractor qualification, takeover mechanisms, competitive
procurement formulas, or procurement scoring.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent clawback formulas, recovery percentages,
collection procedures, penalties, tribunals, contractor-recovery systems,
or enforcement offices.

==================================================
9. TRANSPARENCY AS REPUBLICAN INFRASTRUCTURE
==================================================

A republican government under NBBF SHALL be structurally capable of
exposing its actions for public accountability.

Transparency is infrastructure of republican accountability. It is not
optional ornament.

Detailed API, ledger, identity, database, publication-protocol, and
digital-transparency mechanics: CONTROL 11.

This CONTROL does not create those mechanics.

==================================================
10. CITIZEN TELEMETRY
==================================================

Citizens SHOULD be able to observe meaningful governmental state,
decisions, performance, Signals, and resource movement through authorized
transparency mechanisms.

“Citizen telemetry,” as used here, is that observational principle. It is
not a software product, sensor network, or data standard.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT specify API schemas, endpoints, software,
blockchain, mobile applications, or publication protocols.

Signal operating rules, including non-interference and evidence
preservation: CONTROL 00B.

Publication, API, and ledger mechanics: CONTROL 11.

==================================================
11. REPUBLICAN DIGITAL TWIN
==================================================

Governmental structures and activities MAY eventually be represented
through a sufficiently accurate digital model for public observation and
accountability.

That architectural objective is the Republican Digital Twin requirement
of this CONTROL: purpose and republican-governance necessity only.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT define technical implementation, schemas, runtimes,
identity protocols, or publication systems.

CONTROL 00 states that CDT, UNBKE, artificial intelligence, APIs, ledgers,
runtimes, or other digital implementations SHALL NOT replace human
accountability, lawful human judgment, or constitutional authority.

Digital representation mechanics: CONTROL 11.

==================================================
12. LOCAL CONTROL
==================================================

Legitimate jurisdictional decision authority SHALL be preserved.

A higher jurisdiction SHALL NOT, by NBBF interpretation alone, erase the
lawful local decision authority of a subordinate republican jurisdiction.

Local control does not exempt a jurisdiction from:

• Human accountability under CONTROL 01
• Signal protections under CONTROL 00B
• Property and pooling constraints under CONTROL 00C
• Exit and disengagement under CONTROL 00D
• Cost/value and audit homes under CONTROL 06 and CONTROL 07
• The accountability principles of this CONTROL and CONTROL 00

Local control is not secrecy, unaccountable discretion, or hidden authority.

==================================================
13. NO HIDDEN AUTHORITY
==================================================

Governmental operating authority SHALL NOT arise merely from undocumented
custom, inaccessible process, technological opacity, or administrative
convenience.

Authority that cannot be identified, attributed, and traced through a lawful
delegation path is not legitimate NBBF governmental operating authority.

Classification or sealing may restrict visibility. It SHALL NOT eliminate
accountability. Classified Signal rules: CONTROL 00B. Sealed-Node operating
rules: CONTROL 09. Exit of sealed or classified Nodes: CONTROL 00D.

==================================================
14. TRACEABILITY
==================================================

Governmental authority and material decisions SHALL be capable of tracing
back through their lawful delegation path.

The delegation path includes, as applicable:

• The people of the jurisdiction
• The constitution, charter, or other organic law
• Representative institutions
• The applicable Decision-Node authority
• Human Owners of governmental Nodes
• Authorized executing Nodes

Node ontology of that path: CONTROL 01.
This CONTROL requires that the path be applicable and attributable within
a republican jurisdiction. It does not redefine Node types.

System-level composition of Nodes into larger operating systems: CONTROL 04.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT become a second System Structure Model.

==================================================
15. ENFORCEMENT
==================================================

This CONTROL owns republican-government application rules.

Noncompliance with this CONTROL includes:

• Treating government as independent ownership authority
• Operating a governmental Node without identifiable human accountability
  consistent with CONTROL 01
• Making material governmental decisions that cannot be attributed to an
  identifiable Decision Node and responsible human actor
• Treating this CONTROL as Node ontology, Signal operations, pooling,
  exit, cost/value, audit, procurement, or API mechanics
• Treating this CONTROL as NBBF Root Authority
• Treating this CONTROL as NBEF or as Competitive Node Procurement

Noncompliance MAY be identified by:

• Authorized NBBF CONTROL stewardship under CONTROL 00
• Signals under CONTROL 00B
• Audit under CONTROL 07

Detailed audit procedure: CONTROL 07.
This CONTROL does not create an enforcement agency, tribunal, board,
criminal penalty schedule, or new governmental power.

18. NORTH STAR
==================================================

Authority originates with the people.
Government holds it in trust.
Every public act remains answerable.

No office owns the republic.
No jurisdiction hides its delegation.
No funded act is exempt from attribution.

delegated by CONTROL 00.

==================================================
19. OWNED CONCEPTS
==================================================

This CONTROL is the canonical authority for the NBBF Republican Government
Model, including:

• Application of republican delegated-authority principles to taxpayer-funded
  governmental jurisdictions
• Citizen/taxpayer accountability as an NBBF application principle
• Jurisdictional responsibility and identifiable authority basis
• Attribution of material governmental decisions to Decision Nodes and
  human actors
• Representation as delegated authority rather than system ownership
• Local-control accountability constraints
• No-hidden-authority and delegation-path traceability requirements
• Republican transparency, citizen-telemetry, and Republican Digital Twin
  objectives at the governance-requirement level only

This CONTROL does not own Root authority, Node ontology, Signal operating
rules, property/pooling mechanics, Exit/Disengagement, cost/value, audit
procedure, system composition, review calendar, recovery/clawback,
Legacy/Sealed operating rules, Legislative Decision-Node interface, API or
