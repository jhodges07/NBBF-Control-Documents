12_Competitive_Node_Procurement
Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF)

Version: 1.0.0
Status: Authoritative Control Document
Classification: Canonical
Scope: Specialized rules by which execution of bounded governmental Node
work may be competitively procured while remaining subordinate to the
governmental Node and the existing CONTROL tree, including competition,
anti-capture, observability, replaceability, prime/subcontractor
visibility, and preservation of Human Owner accountability.

Lineage:
This CONTROL is the Human Engineer-authorized first canonical draft of
CONTROL 12, executed under NBBF-CWC #29 pursuant to NBBF-CWC #16 foundation
certification, NBBF-CWC #17 assignment of Competitive Node Procurement as
CONTROL 12, NBBF-CWC #19 Root reserved-home recognition, and subsequent
establishment of CONTROLs 03 through 11. Metadata describes authority.
Metadata does not independently create authority.

AUTHORITATIVE HOME
==================================================

This CONTROL answers:

HOW MAY EXECUTION OF BOUNDED GOVERNMENTAL NODE WORK BE COMPETITIVELY
PROCURED WITHOUT CONVERTING THE PERFORMER INTO GOVERNMENTAL AUTHORITY
AND WITHOUT DEFEATING ACCOUNTABILITY, COMPETITION, OBSERVABILITY, OR
REPLACEABILITY?

CONTROL 00 answers: WHAT IS NBBF ROOT AUTHORITY?
CONTROL 01 answers: WHAT IS A NODE AND WHO IS THE HUMAN OWNER?
CONTROL 04 answers: HOW ARE AUTHORIZED NODES COMPOSED INTO A SYSTEM?
CONTROL 05 answers: WHEN DOES AUTHORITY REVIEW OCCUR?
CONTROL 08 answers: HOW DOES RECOVERY AND CLAWBACK OPERATE?
CONTROL 11 answers: HOW IS PUBLIC INFORMATION PUBLISHED?

This CONTROL SHALL reference those authorities where required.
It SHALL NOT duplicate them.

Cross-reference ≠ authority transfer.

Concise vocabulary: CONTROL 02.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes Competitive Node Procurement under NBBF.

Its purpose is to permit lawful competitive procurement of bounded
execution while preserving:

• Governmental authority
• Human Owner accountability
• Node boundaries
• Measurable performance
• Competitive access
• Resistance to contractor capture
• Failure containment
• Replaceability
• Continuity
• Signals
• Review
• Audit
• Recovery
• Exit
• Legacy/Sealed treatment
• Transparency
• Property rights
• Historical lineage

Procurement SHALL remain subordinate to the governmental Node and the
existing CONTROL tree.

==================================================
2. PROCUREMENT IS EXECUTION, NOT SOVEREIGN AUTHORITY
==================================================

The following distinctions SHALL be preserved:

• Contract ≠ governmental authority
• Payment ≠ governmental authority
• Expertise ≠ governmental authority
• Incumbency ≠ governmental authority
• Possession of government systems ≠ governmental authority
• Possession of government data ≠ governmental authority
• Contract award ≠ performance proof
• Contractor ≠ Human Owner

A contractor may execute work.

A contractor SHALL NOT acquire the Node’s authority by executing that
work, holding systems, holding data, being paid, or remaining the
incumbent.

Award of a contract SHALL NOT create a Node type, enlarge Node scope,
or substitute for Decision-Node authorization.

Node types, Human Owner, and Decision Node remain CONTROL 01.
Republican-government application remains CONTROL 03.
Legislative Decision-Node interface remains CONTROL 10.

==================================================
3. FAIL-CLOSED SEMANTICS
==================================================

The following distinctions SHALL be preserved:

• Unknown ≠ qualified
• Unknown ≠ unqualified unless applicable authority establishes that
  result
• Unknown ≠ compliant
• Unverified ≠ verified
• Bid submitted ≠ qualified
• Contract awarded ≠ authority created
• Contract signed ≠ performance proven
• Incumbent ≠ preferred
• Lowest price ≠ automatically best outcome
• Failure ≠ automatically fraud
• Confidential ≠ sealed
• Proprietary ≠ exempt from public accountability
• Legacy ≠ authorized

This CONTROL SHALL NOT fill evidentiary gaps for convenience.

Unknown SHALL remain unknown.

==================================================
4. HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY
==================================================

CONTROL 01 remains authoritative for Human Owner.

Procurement SHALL NOT allow governmental accountability to disappear
into:

• Corporations
• Limited-liability companies
• Vendors
• Prime contractors
• Subcontractors
• Committees
• Procurement offices
• Consultants
• Software
• Algorithms
• Artificial intelligence
• Anonymous project teams

A lawful governmental accountability chain SHALL remain identifiable.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent a second Human Owner model.

A publication custodian, implementer, prime, subcontractor, or automated
system is not the Human Owner of the procured Node unless that person
is already the Human Owner under CONTROL 01.

==================================================
5. NODE-BOUNDED PROCUREMENT
==================================================

CONTROL 01 remains Node ontology.
CONTROL 04 remains the System Structure Model.

Procurement SHOULD be bounded so that:

• The work being purchased is identifiable
• Responsibility is traceable
• Performance can be observed
• Failure can be localized
• Affected work can be distinguished from unaffected work
• Replacement of a performer need not unnecessarily collapse a larger
  governmental system

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a new Node type.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent arbitrary numerical Node-size limits.

The architectural objective is localized failure:

FAIL SMALL WHERE REASONABLY GOVERNABLE.

That phrase is an architectural objective. It is not a numerical rule.

A procurement that cannot be attached to an identifiable Node is
structurally defective under this CONTROL.

Composition of Nodes into systems remains CONTROL 04. A contractor
appearing in a System Structure remains attached to an identifiable
Node. That appearance is not itself a procurement award.

==================================================
6. REAL COMPETITION
==================================================

Competition SHALL protect the public from capture and dependency.

Competition SHALL NOT be merely ceremonial.

Procurement architecture SHALL NOT be deliberately structured so that
an incumbent becomes the only practically capable future performer
because government allowed avoidable dependency to accumulate.

Structural risks that this CONTROL addresses include:

• Incumbent lock-in
• Proprietary dependency
• Inaccessible documentation
• Undocumented operational knowledge
• Artificially bundled scope
• Artificial qualification barriers
• Hidden selection criteria
• Contractor-created opacity
• Dependence on inaccessible proprietary systems
• Intentional barriers to transition

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent statutory bidding thresholds.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent Kansas procurement law.

Incumbency SHALL NOT itself create preference.

==================================================
7. NEW ENTRANTS AND SMALLER PERFORMERS
==================================================

Competitive Node Procurement SHOULD allow qualified new entrants and
smaller performers to compete for appropriately bounded work.

This SHALL NOT mean lowering legitimate performance requirements.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create:

• Quotas
• Protected-class preferences
• Arbitrary set-asides
• Guaranteed awards
• Political favoritism
• Punishment of a firm merely because it is an incumbent

Qualification SHOULD relate to the actual bounded work and required
evidence.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent qualification formulas.

Unknown ≠ qualified.

==================================================
8. PRIME AND SUBCONTRACTOR STRUCTURES
==================================================

Prime and subcontractor arrangements SHALL NOT create opacity.

A prime contractor SHALL NOT become an opaque shell behind which
material execution relationships disappear where the existing CONTROL
tree requires those relationships to remain observable.

Subcontracting SHALL NOT silently:

• Erase responsibility
• Erase Signals
• Erase evidence
• Defeat audit
• Defeat recovery
• Defeat transparency
• Manufacture secrecy
• Conceal material conflicts
• Create governmental authority

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create detailed subcontracting regulations
beyond the observability and anti-opacity requirements of this
CONTROL and the homes it references.

Material execution relationships remain attachable to identifiable
Nodes and Human Owners. CONTROL 01 and CONTROL 04 remain those homes
for ontology and composition.

==================================================
9. PERFORMANCE OBSERVABILITY
==================================================

Procured work SHALL remain compatible with existing mechanisms for:

• Signals — CONTROL 00B
• Review — CONTROL 05
• Audit — CONTROL 07
• Recovery — CONTROL 08
• Transparency — CONTROL 11
• Lineage — CONTROL 00 and applicable CONTROLs
• Structural accountability — CONTROL 01 and CONTROL 04

This CONTROL SHALL NOT duplicate those mechanisms.

A procurement instrument SHALL NOT give a contractor authority to
suppress, destroy, rewrite, conceal, or improperly delay evidence or
Signals governed elsewhere.

Contract award ≠ performance proof.
Contract signed ≠ performance proven.

==================================================
10. FAILURE AND REPLACEABILITY
==================================================

Procurement SHALL NOT intentionally create a performer that becomes
practically irreplaceable through avoidable dependency.

This CONTROL SHOULD establish structural requirements supporting:

• Transition readiness
• Adequate documentation
• Continuity of public operations
• Preservation of government-controlled authoritative records
• Identification of affected Node work
• Preservation of relevant history
• Separation of contractor property from governmental authority
• Successor accessibility to lawfully transferable information
• Reasonable ability to transfer bounded execution

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent:

• Automatic contractor replacement
• A performer-failure trigger calendar
• Mandatory standby contractors
• A detailed takeover procedure
• Replacement algorithms
• Automatic termination
• Automatic successor selection

Those mechanisms remain deferred unless separately authorized.

Replaceability is a structural condition: the public SHALL NOT be
locked to an avoidably irreplaceable performer. The procedure for
selecting a successor remains outside this CONTROL except as later
authorized.

Recovery from a contractor remains CONTROL 08. This CONTROL SHALL NOT
select a replacement provider by recovery doctrine.

==================================================
11. SIGNAL BOUNDARY
==================================================

CONTROL 00B owns Signal operations.

Procurement SHALL NOT be used to:

• Suppress Signals
• Erase Signals
• Manufacture Signal closure
• Prohibit authorized reporting
• Divert required Signals into unauthorized private-only channels
• Create contractual interference with lawful Signal operation

Contractors and vendors remain among those with reporting rights under
CONTROL 00B. This CONTROL SHALL NOT weaken those rights.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT duplicate CONTROL 00B.

==================================================
12. REVIEW BOUNDARY
==================================================

CONTROL 05 remains the clock.

Procurement MAY remain subject to review authority established by
CONTROL 05.

This CONTROL MAY require that procured execution remain reviewable.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT define the review cadence, review-due dates, or
reauthorization calendar.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a competing review clock.

==================================================
13. AUDIT BOUNDARY
==================================================

CONTROL 07 owns audit.

Contractor possession, proprietary systems, subcontracting, or private
records SHALL NOT automatically extinguish otherwise authorized audit.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a second audit system.

==================================================
14. RECOVERY AND CLAWBACK BOUNDARY
==================================================

CONTROL 08 owns recovery and clawback.

A procurement instrument SHALL NOT silently immunize a performer from
otherwise applicable recovery authority.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create:

• Recovery formulas
• Taxpayer-clawback formulas
• Repayment percentages
• Damages formulas
• Collection systems
• Automatic contractor debt

CONTROL 08 MAY govern recovery from a contractor when a valid recovery
right exists. That fact SHALL NOT turn this CONTROL into a second
recovery CONTROL, and SHALL NOT turn CONTROL 08 into procurement.

==================================================
15. EXIT BOUNDARY
==================================================

CONTROL 00D owns Exit and Disengagement.

Procurement termination and CONTROL 00D Exit SHALL NOT automatically
be treated as the same thing.

Procurement SHALL NOT erase Exit evidence, rights, or history governed
elsewhere.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a second Exit mechanism.

==================================================
16. LEGACY AND SEALED BOUNDARY
==================================================

CONTROL 09 remains authoritative for Legacy and Sealed treatment.

A contractor SHALL NOT manufacture governmental sealing authority by
asserting:

• Confidentiality
• Trade secret
• Proprietary information
• Security
• Contractual restriction

Lawful restrictions remain lawful restrictions.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent secrecy law.

Confidential ≠ sealed.
Proprietary ≠ exempt from public accountability.

Existing contractor relationships SHALL NOT automatically become
Legacy merely because they predate this CONTROL.

Where CONTROL 09 legitimately applies, Legacy treatment does not
automatically:

• Validate authority
• Waive competition
• Waive review
• Waive Signals
• Waive audit
• Waive recovery
• Waive transparency
• Create permanent incumbency

Legacy ≠ authorized.

==================================================
17. TRANSPARENCY BOUNDARY
==================================================

CONTROL 11 owns Public Transparency API and Publishing.

Procurement SHALL remain compatible with lawful public transparency.

A contractor SHALL NOT acquire an opacity privilege merely by placing
publicly accountable work inside:

• Proprietary software
• Private databases
• Proprietary formats
• Inaccessible documentation
• Private dashboards
• Subcontracting structures

This CONTROL SHALL NOT mandate REST, GraphQL, JSON, SQL, blockchain,
or any specific technical implementation.

Machine accessibility and human readability remain CONTROL 11.
Publication is representation, not authority.

==================================================
18. PROPERTY RIGHTS
==================================================

CONTROL 00C remains authoritative for property and pooling.

This CONTROL SHALL respect legitimate contractor property rights.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT assume that everything created, used, or
possessed by a contractor automatically becomes government property.

Contractor property claims SHALL NOT silently transform
government-owned authoritative records or lawful public accountability
into private contractor property.

Where ownership is unresolved:

UNKNOWN SHALL REMAIN UNKNOWN.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent ownership.

Cost and Value remain CONTROL 06. Compete-on-value under CONTROL 06 is
not this CONTROL.

==================================================
19. ANTI-CAPTURE AND ANTI-FAVORITISM
==================================================

Competitive procurement SHALL NOT be converted into hidden favoritism.

At minimum this CONTROL addresses:

• Undisclosed material conflicts
• Hidden side agreements affecting selection or performance
• Hidden evaluation criteria
• Criteria manipulated to predetermine a winner
• Artificial qualification barriers created solely to exclude otherwise
  qualified competition
• Undisclosed preferential treatment
• Contractor control over the rules by which its own performance is
  judged
• Political favoritism
• Incumbent-created dependency used to defeat future competition

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent criminal statutes, ethics offenses,
protest procedures, or Kansas-specific remedies.

Material selection criteria SHOULD be knowable and reviewable under
applicable authority.

==================================================
20. PRICE AND SELECTION
==================================================

This CONTROL SHALL NOT assume that lowest nominal price automatically
equals best public outcome unless superior authority requires that
result.

Lowest price ≠ automatically best outcome.

Where lawfully applicable, evaluation MAY distinguish relevant,
measurable factors such as:

• Capability
• Demonstrated performance
• Continuity risk
• Transition readiness
• Documentation
• Dependency risk
• Transparency compatibility
• Audit compatibility
• Total public obligation

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent scoring weights.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT replace transparent criteria with undefined
discretionary “best value.”

Material selection criteria SHOULD be knowable and reviewable under
applicable authority.

The authorized governmental decision remains human. CONTROL 01 and
CONTROL 10 remain the homes for Human Owner and Decision-Node
interface as applicable.

==================================================
21. HUMAN SELECTION AUTHORITY AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
==================================================

Artificial intelligence and automated systems MAY assist with:

• Organizing submissions
• Comparisons
• Search
• Summarization
• Anomaly detection
• Identifying inconsistencies
• Identifying missing evidence
• Presentation

Artificial intelligence SHALL NOT silently:

• Create procurement authority
• Award a contract as sovereign authority
• Become Human Owner
• Fabricate qualification evidence
• Alter authoritative submissions
• Fill unknown facts
• Hide conflicts
• Waive requirements
• Suppress Signals
• Invent performance history
• Declare compliance where human authority is required

Where material, automated analysis SHOULD remain distinguishable from
the authorized governmental decision.

==================================================
22. ANTI-OPACITY
==================================================

Procurement architecture SHALL NOT be used to create dark governmental
execution.

This CONTROL prohibits use of procurement architecture to create:

• Opaque prime-contractor structures
• Invisible material subcontracting
• Hidden performance failures
• Inaccessible public records
• Avoidable proprietary lock-in
• Undocumented dependency
• Hidden side agreements
• Hidden conflicts
• Manipulated selection criteria
• Artificial barriers protecting favored firms
• Erased predecessor history
• Anonymous responsibility
• Aggregation designed to hide Node-level failure
• Fragmentation designed to evade accountability

Aggregation SHALL NOT hide Node-level failure.
Fragmentation SHALL NOT evade accountability.

==================================================

==================================================

This CONTROL v1.0.0 defines architecture, not software.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT design:

• Procurement applications
• Bidding portals
• Databases
• API endpoints
• JSON schemas
• Blockchain
• Smart contracts
• Dashboards
• AI agents
• Authentication systems
• Payment systems
• Scoring applications

==================================================
24. JURISDICTION AND NBEF
==================================================

This CONTROL is jurisdiction-neutral.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent Kansas procurement statutes,
constitutional rules, regulatory thresholds, bidding thresholds,
criminal offenses, remedies, protest rights, or appeal procedures.

Future legislation implementing this CONTROL must separately reconcile
the architecture against controlling law.

INFORMATIVE: a state, county, municipality, township, school district,
special district, or other lawful taxpayer-funded jurisdiction MAY
procure bounded Node execution under these rules. These examples do
not create jurisdiction-specific governmental authority.

This CONTROL does not create NBEF.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create curriculum, student, teacher,
educational-delivery, or other education-domain operating doctrine.

Republican-government application remains CONTROL 03.

==================================================
25. ENFORCEMENT
==================================================

This CONTROL owns Competitive Node Procurement rules.

Noncompliance includes:

• Treating a contract, payment, expertise, incumbency, or possession of
  systems or data as governmental authority
• Substituting a contractor, corporation, committee, office, software,
  or AI for the Human Owner
• Using procurement to suppress Signals, defeat audit, immunize
  recovery, erase Exit history, or manufacture sealing
• Structuring procurement so an incumbent becomes avoidably
  irreplaceable
• Using ceremonial competition, hidden criteria, or artificial barriers
  to predetermine a winner
• Using prime/subcontractor structure to erase material relationships
• Treating this CONTROL as Signal operations, property/pooling, Exit,
  ontology, composition, review calendar, cost/value, audit, recovery,
  Legacy/Sealed treatment, Decision-Node interface, publication, NBEF,
  or Root authority

Detection MAY occur through:

• Authorized NBBF CONTROL stewardship under CONTROL 00
• Signals under CONTROL 00B
• Review under CONTROL 05
• Audit under CONTROL 07

Detailed audit procedure: CONTROL 07.
This CONTROL does not create an enforcement agency, tribunal, board,
criminal penalty, civil penalty, or new governmental power.

==================================================
26. EXIT AUTHORITY
==================================================

This CONTROL does not own operational Exit.

Distinguish:

A. Competitive Node Procurement — this CONTROL
B. Operational Exit and Disengagement — CONTROL 00D
C. Recovery and clawback — CONTROL 08
D. Review and reauthorization — CONTROL 05
E. Procurement-instrument termination — a contractual or lawful ending
   of a procurement relationship, which is not automatically CONTROL 00D
   Exit
F. CONTROL-document supersession — CONTROL 00 versioned replacement

These SHALL NOT silently collapse into one process.

Replacement or ending of a performer SHALL NOT silently erase lawfully
required history.

29. OWNED CONCEPTS
==================================================

This CONTROL is the canonical authority for Competitive Node
Procurement, including:

• Procurement as execution, not sovereign authority
• Preservation of Human Owner accountability through procured execution
• Node-bounded procurement without a new Node ontology
• Substantive competition and constraint of avoidable incumbent lock-in
• Access of qualified new and smaller performers without quotas or
  guaranteed awards
• Prime/subcontractor observability without detailed subcontracting

• Compatibility of procured work with Signal, review, audit, recovery,

• Anti-capture and anti-favoritism architecture
• Price and selection without scoring-weight formulas
• Human selection authority and the AI-assistance boundary
• Anti-opacity rules against dark governmental execution
• Distinguishing contractor property from governmental authority
• Implementation-neutral procurement architecture

This CONTROL does not own Root authority, Signal operations,
property/pooling, Exit/Disengagement, Node ontology, general
vocabulary, republican-government application, system composition,
review calendar, cost/value, audit/performance, recovery/clawback,
Legacy/Sealed treatment, Legislative Decision-Node interface, public
transparency publication, or NBEF.
