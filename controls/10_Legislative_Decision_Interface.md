10_Legislative_Decision_Interface
Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF)

Version: 1.0.0
Status: Authoritative Control Document
Classification: Canonical
Scope: Interface between NBBF and lawful legislative or equivalent
governmental Decision Nodes, including how a matter requiring such
authorization enters, receives attributable human decision, is recorded,
and leaves that decision process.

Lineage:

CONTROL 10, executed under NBBF-CWC #23 pursuant to NBBF-CWC #16 foundation
certification, NBBF-CWC #17 Decision 6 partial empty-CONTROL release, and
NBBF-CWC #19 Root reserved-home recognition. Metadata describes authority.

==================================================
AUTHORITATIVE HOME
==================================================

This CONTROL answers:

HOW DOES AN NBBF MATTER REQUIRING LEGISLATIVE OR EQUIVALENT
DECISION-NODE AUTHORITY ENTER, RECEIVE ATTRIBUTABLE HUMAN DECISION,
AND LEAVE THAT DECISION PROCESS?

CONTROL 01 answers: WHAT IS A DECISION NODE?
CONTROL 03 answers: HOW DOES NBBF APPLY REPUBLICAN GOVERNMENT PRINCIPLES?
CONTROL 05 answers: WHEN DOES AUTHORITY REVIEW OCCUR AND WHEN IS IT DUE?

This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a second review calendar.
CONTROL 05 remains the clock.
This CONTROL remains the Decision-Node interface.

Concise vocabulary: CONTROL 02.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes the Legislative Decision Interface of NBBF.

It defines how a Decision Node receives, considers, attributes, records,
and disposes of matters requiring lawful legislative or equivalent
governmental authorization.

It SHALL NOT dictate the internal rules of a legislature beyond the NBBF
accountability requirements lawfully applicable to the Decision Node.

It SHALL NOT replace constitutional government.

==================================================
2. JURISDICTION NEUTRALITY
==================================================

This CONTROL is jurisdiction-neutral.

A Decision Node MAY include, where lawfully applicable:

• Congress
• A state legislature
• A legislative chamber
• A county commission
• A city council
• Another constitutionally or statutorily authorized governmental body

Congress is an example of a Decision-Node authority in the United States
federal legislative jurisdiction. Congress is not the universal or exclusive
NBBF Decision Node.

Decision Node ontology: CONTROL 01.
Republican-government application: CONTROL 03.

School districts or other education-related governing bodies MAY appear
only as jurisdictional examples. They do not establish NBEF.

==================================================
3. DECISION-NODE INPUT
==================================================

Authorized matters MAY reach a Decision Node through this interface.

Potential inputs MAY include:

• CONTROL 05 scheduled reauthorization
• CONTROL 00B escalated Signals
• CONTROL 07 audit or performance findings
• CONTROL 08 recovery matters requiring lawful authorization
• CONTROL 00D matters requiring Decision-Node action
• CONTROL 06 cost/value information
• Other lawful governmental referrals

An input is not a decision.
A Signal is not a vote.
An audit finding is not legislation.
A recommendation is not authorization.

==================================================
4. DECISION PACKAGE
==================================================

A material matter submitted through this interface SHOULD be capable of
identifying at minimum:

• Affected Node
• Matter requiring decision
• Lawful authority being invoked
• Responsible Decision Node
• Accountable human participants where legally identifiable
• Originating CONTROL or referral
• Relevant Signals
• Relevant audit or performance findings
• Cost/value information
• Recovery or exit implications where applicable
• Alternatives presented
• Material known consequences
• Required decision date
• Resulting disposition

This CONTROL does not create API schemas, endpoints, or publication
protocols. Digital representation and publication mechanics: CONTROL 11,
reserved.

==================================================
5. ATTRIBUTABLE HUMAN DECISION
==================================================

Every material Decision-Node disposition SHALL remain attributable to
lawful human governmental authority.

Where the governing body acts collectively, attribution SHALL preserve
the legally meaningful collective action and, where lawfully public,
individual votes or other attributable actions.

Software MAY assist.
Artificial intelligence MAY assist.

Neither software nor artificial intelligence MAY:

• Cast a governmental vote
• Silently create consent
• Silently reauthorize authority
• Conceal the responsible human authority
• Replace constitutional decision-making

Human Owner meaning: CONTROL 01.
Republican human accountability: CONTROL 03.

==================================================
6. VOTE AND DECISION RECORD
==================================================

Where a formal vote is legally required, this interface SHALL preserve
the existence and result of that vote.

Where individual votes are legally public, NBBF SHOULD preserve their
attribution.

Where another lawful decision mechanism applies, this interface SHALL
identify that mechanism and the resulting human or governmental authority.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent voting requirements where law does not
require a vote.

==================================================
7. SILENCE IS NOT AUTHORIZATION
==================================================

Silence SHALL NOT constitute authorization.

Failure to vote SHALL NOT silently become approval.

Expiration of a review date SHALL NOT silently become reauthorization.

Software timeout SHALL NOT create governmental consent.

Budget continuation SHALL NOT automatically satisfy a separate
reauthorization requirement established under NBBF.

Compatible principle: CONTROL 05. This CONTROL SHALL NOT create a second
review calendar.

==================================================
8. CONTROL 05 REAUTHORIZATION INTERFACE
==================================================

CONTROL 05 owns review cadence, monthly operational review, four-month
authority and performance review, review-due dates, and reauthorization
disposition.

This CONTROL owns the legislative Decision-Node interface when that
reauthorization requires legislative or equivalent governmental action.

CONTROL 05 remains the clock.
This CONTROL remains the interface.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent CONTROL 05 doctrine.

==================================================
9. CLASSIFIED AND RESTRICTED REAUTHORIZATION
==================================================

CONTROL 00B owns classified Signal operating doctrine.
CONTROL 05 owns the review calendar.
This CONTROL owns the Decision-Node interface used when lawful
reauthorization of continued secrecy or classified treatment requires
Decision-Node action.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT duplicate CONTROL 00B classified-reporting
mechanics.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent classification authority.

Secrecy SHALL NOT become permanent merely through silence or
administrative inertia.

==================================================
10. DECISION DISPOSITIONS
==================================================

This CONTROL MAY record lawful Decision-Node outcomes such as:

• APPROVED
• APPROVED WITH LAWFUL CONDITIONS
• MODIFIED
• REJECTED
• RETURNED FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
• REFERRED TO ANOTHER LAWFUL AUTHORITY
• NO DECISION / UNRESOLVED

Use only dispositions supportable within existing lawful authority.

NO DECISION / UNRESOLVED SHALL NOT silently equal approval.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent penalties or governmental powers.

==================================================
11. PERMANENT DECISION HISTORY
==================================================

Decision history SHALL remain traceable.

A later decision SHALL NOT erase:

• Prior votes
• Prior dispositions
• Prior Signals
• Prior audit findings
• Prior review outcomes
• Prior recovery findings
• Prior material alternatives
• Prior accountable decision authority

Close is not erase.

Compatible principle: CONTROL 00B CLOSE DOES NOT MEAN ERASE. This CONTROL
does not duplicate Signal-close operating doctrine.

Ledger, API, and publication mechanics: CONTROL 11, reserved.

==================================================
12. TAXPAYER VISIBILITY
==================================================

Decision-Node status, lawful inputs, dispositions, and attributable
public votes SHOULD be visible to taxpayers to the maximum extent
permitted by constitutional or statutory authority.

This is a governance requirement.

CONTROL 11 owns API, publication, ledger, digital-identity, and
Republican Digital Twin implementation mechanics. Those mechanics are
reserved and are not drafted here.

==================================================
13. CITIZEN TELEMETRY
==================================================

This CONTROL SHOULD support the future ability of citizens to observe:

• What decision is pending
• Which Node is affected
• Why the decision is required
• When it is due
• Which lawful authority owns the decision
• What evidence is before the Decision Node
• What disposition occurred
• Who was accountable where legally public

Citizen telemetry, as used here, is an accountability principle. It is
not surveillance of private citizens.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT authorize disclosure of protected personal
information.

Implementation: CONTROL 11, reserved.

==================================================
14. REPUBLICAN DIGITAL TWIN
==================================================

This CONTROL SHALL be capable of future representation within a
Republican Digital Twin.

The Digital Twin MAY show the lawful decision pathway.

It SHALL NOT become the decision-maker.

Digital representation SHALL NOT replace elected representatives, lawful
officials, constitutional institutions, or accountable human authority.

Implementation: CONTROL 11, reserved.

==================================================
15. SIGNAL BOUNDARY
==================================================

Signal operating doctrine: CONTROL 00B.

This CONTROL MAY receive matters surfaced by Signals.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine reporting rights, Signal evidence,
escalation, suppression, interference, Signal close, or Signal lifecycle.

==================================================
16. AUDIT BOUNDARY
==================================================

Audit procedure and performance findings: CONTROL 07.

This CONTROL MAY receive those findings.

A finding SHALL NOT automatically dictate a legislative decision unless
superior law independently requires that result.

Decision accountability remains human and lawful.

==================================================
17. RECOVERY BOUNDARY
==================================================

Recovery and clawback mechanics: CONTROL 08.

This CONTROL MAY provide lawful Decision-Node authorization where such

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent clawback formulas, recovery percentages,
collection procedures, contractor penalties, recovery tribunals, or
recovery offices.

Exit and Disengagement: CONTROL 00D.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT become a second Exit CONTROL.

==================================================
20. NBEF BOUNDARY
==================================================

This CONTROL does not create NBEF.

==================================================
21. ENFORCEMENT
==================================================

Noncompliance with this CONTROL includes:

• Treating silence as approval
• Hiding responsible Decision-Node authority
• Allowing software or artificial intelligence to create governmental
  authorization
• Erasing material decision history
• Misrepresenting a recommendation as authorization
• Treating a Signal or audit finding as a completed legislative decision
• Using this CONTROL to invent governmental powers
• Concealing legally public decision attribution
• Treating this CONTROL as review calendar, Exit, recovery, procurement,
  or Root authority

Noncompliance MAY be identified by:

• Authorized NBBF CONTROL stewardship under CONTROL 00
• Signals under CONTROL 00B
• Audit under CONTROL 07
• Authority review under CONTROL 05

Detailed audit procedure: CONTROL 07.
This CONTROL does not create an enforcement agency, tribunal, board,
criminal penalty schedule, or new governmental power.
