==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes recovery and clawback operating rules of NBBF.

Taxpayer resources do not lose accountability merely because government has
transferred, paid, advanced, allocated, contracted, granted, or otherwise
moved them.

Where law, agreement, contract, grant condition, program rule, or other
valid authority creates a recovery right, NBBF SHALL preserve the visibility
and traceability necessary to exercise that right.

Recovery SHALL be evidence-based, attributable, reviewable, proportionate
to the authorized recovery basis, and visible through the appropriate
transparency mechanisms.

This CONTROL defines NBBF recovery states and requirements. It does not
claim that NBBF itself supplies governmental legal authority. Recovery
authority MUST have a lawful basis outside this CONTROL.

==================================================
2. CORE DISTINCTIONS
==================================================

This CONTROL distinguishes:

A. RECOVERY — return, offset, restoration, or other authorized recapture of
   amounts or value supported by a lawful recovery basis.
B. PENALTY — an additional sanction authorized independently of ordinary
   recovery.
C. DAMAGES — a separately authorized compensatory or other damage award.
D. CRIMINAL CONSEQUENCE — criminal process and punishment under criminal
   law.

This CONTROL owns recovery mechanics. It does not create criminal law,
penalty schedules, or damage measures.

A Signal alone SHALL NOT automatically equal a final recovery determination.

An audit finding alone SHALL NOT automatically create legal recovery
authority where none exists.

Evidence and referral are not determination.

==================================================
3. CLAWBACK
==================================================

Clawback is a recovery mechanism available only when supported by valid
authority.

Clawback MAY address, where applicable and authorized:

• Amounts paid for work not performed
• Amounts attributable to materially deficient performance
• Unsupported or improperly documented payments
• Duplicate or erroneous payments
• Advances not earned
• Taxpayer-funded assets or resources improperly retained
• Recoverable benefits arising from misrepresentation or breach
• Other specifically authorized recovery claims

Every performance failure SHALL NOT be presumed to produce a 100% clawback.

This CONTROL does not invent arbitrary percentages, schedules, or formulas.
The recoverable amount is determined from evidence and the lawful recovery
basis under §6.

==================================================
4. RECOVERY LIFECYCLE
==================================================

A recovery matter under this CONTROL SHALL be capable of occupying the
following NBBF states as applicable:

1. Potential Recovery Condition
2. Referral
3. Evidence Preservation
4. Recovery Review
5. Recovery Determination
6. Recoverable Amount / Recoverable Value Determination
7. Notice or other required lawful process
8. Recovery Action
9. Collection / Offset / Return / Restoration where authorized
10. Dispute or review status where applicable
11. Resolution
12. Close
13. Continuing record / historical traceability

These are NBBF operating states. They do not create a court, tribunal,
agency, board, or office.

A matter MAY conclude at Recovery Review or Recovery Determination with
NO RECOVERY AUTHORIZED under §12.

==================================================
5. TRIGGERS AND REFERRAL
==================================================

This CONTROL MAY receive properly authorized referrals arising from:

• CONTROL 07 audit findings
• CONTROL 06 cost/value evidence
• CONTROL 00B Signals
• Authorized review processes, including an adverse authorized review
• Lawful contractual conditions
• Grant conditions
• Documented nonperformance
• Improper payment
• Duplicate payment
• Unsupported payment
• Misrepresentation
• Failure to satisfy an authorized condition
• Unearned advance
• Unused or improperly retained taxpayer resources
• Other legally valid recovery conditions

An authorized review MAY trigger recovery review. Calendar, cadence,
reauthorization, and periodic-review mechanics belong to CONTROL 05.
CONTROL 05 remains reserved and is not drafted here.

Until CONTROL 05 exists, this CONTROL SHALL refer to an “authorized
review.” It SHALL NOT invent CONTROL 05 doctrine, including any
four-month or other calendar.

Referral opens Recovery Review. Referral does not itself determine
recoverable value or authorize collection.

==================================================
6. RECOVERABLE VALUE
==================================================

Recoverable value SHALL be determined from preserved evidence and the
lawful recovery basis.

The determination SHOULD be traceable to evidence and MAY consider:

• Amount paid
• Amount properly earned
• Value actually delivered
• Value not delivered
• Corrective costs where lawfully recoverable
• Duplicated amounts
• Improperly retained amounts
• Unused advances
• Identifiable taxpayer loss
• Offsets or credits
• Amounts already returned
• Other legally authorized recovery components

Value meaning: CONTROL 06. This CONTROL SHALL use CONTROL 06 concepts
where appropriate. This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine Value.

Cost and parked-balance measurement: CONTROL 06.
Property and pooling: CONTROL 00C.

==================================================
7. PROPORTIONALITY
==================================================

Recovery SHALL NOT automatically exceed the amount or value supported by
the lawful recovery basis.

This CONTROL rejects punitive multiplication masquerading as ordinary
recovery unless separately authorized by applicable law.

Penalties, damages, interest, fees, or similar additions MAY be recognized
only where independently authorized.

An ordinary performance disagreement, standing alone, is not a lawful
recovery basis and SHALL NOT be converted into confiscation through this
CONTROL.

==================================================
8. EVIDENCE
==================================================

Recovery SHALL be tied to preserved evidence.

The recovery record SHOULD identify, as applicable:

• Affected Node
• Responsible Human Owner or accountable actor where established
• Decision Node where relevant
• Underlying transaction or resource movement
• Audit finding
• Signal references
• Agreement, contract, or grant authority
• Performance evidence
• Amount/value calculation
• Recovery authority
• Recovery status
• Disposition

This CONTROL consumes authorized evidence. It SHALL NOT duplicate CONTROL
00B Signal evidence lifecycle or CONTROL 07 audit procedure.

Evidence attached to a recovery matter SHALL NOT be altered, substituted,
concealed, or destroyed without preservation of the original record.

==================================================
9. HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY
==================================================

Automated systems MAY calculate, surface, organize, or track recovery
information.

Automated systems SHALL NOT silently create recovery authority.

Material recovery determinations SHALL remain attributable to authorized
human decision authority unless applicable law expressly provides
otherwise.

Human Owner and governmental accountability: CONTROL 01 and CONTROL 03.
This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine those homes.

==================================================
10. PROPERTY-RIGHTS SAFEGUARD
==================================================

This CONTROL SHALL respect CONTROL 00C.

Recovery doctrine SHALL NOT be used as a pretext for unauthorized taking,
pooling, confiscation, forfeiture, or reassignment of private property.

Government MUST identify the lawful recovery basis.

NBBF recovery traceability does not itself create ownership.

==================================================
11. NOTICE, ACTION, AND DISPUTE
==================================================

Where law, agreement, or governing procedure requires notice or other
process before recovery action, that process SHALL be observed and
recorded.

Recovery Action, Collection, Offset, Return, or Restoration MAY occur only
where authorized.

Where law, agreement, or governing procedure provides a dispute, appeal,
administrative review, judicial review, or other challenge mechanism, this
CONTROL SHALL preserve the status and traceability of that process.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent a new tribunal or appeal body.

==================================================
12. NO RECOVERY AUTHORIZED
==================================================

A Recovery Review or Recovery Determination SHOULD be capable of concluding
that no recovery is authorized.

When evidence or legal authority is insufficient, the matter SHALL be
capable of returning:

NO RECOVERY AUTHORIZED

The absence of recovery SHALL NOT require deletion of the underlying audit
or Signal record.

==================================================
13. NO SIDE DEALS
==================================================

Recovery obligations SHALL NOT be silently waived, reduced, reassigned,
concealed, or extinguished through undocumented side agreements.

Any authorized compromise, settlement, write-off, waiver, or adjustment
SHALL be:

• Attributable to lawful authority
• Attributable to responsible human decision-makers
• Documented
• Traceable
• Subject to applicable transparency and audit requirements

An undocumented administrative decision is not a lawful extinguishment
under this CONTROL.

==================================================
14. CLOSE DOES NOT MEAN ERASE
==================================================

Closing a recovery process SHALL NOT silently erase:

• The underlying finding
• The recovery basis
• Amounts determined
• Amounts recovered
• Unresolved amounts
• Disposition
• Material decisions

A resolved recovery matter SHALL retain historical traceability consistent
with CONTROL 00 lineage principles and applicable record requirements.

Compatible principle: CONTROL 00B CLOSE DOES NOT MEAN ERASE. This CONTROL
does not duplicate Signal-close operating doctrine.

==================================================
15. EXIT BOUNDARY
==================================================

Operating Exit and Disengagement: CONTROL 00D.

A Node, contractor, recipient, program, or other participant MAY be
subject to both:

• An Exit/Disengagement process under CONTROL 00D; and
• A Recovery process under this CONTROL.

Those are distinct processes.

EXIT DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY ERASE AN OTHERWISE VALID RECOVERY CLAIM.

RECOVERY DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY AUTHORIZE EXIT.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT redefine Exit.

==================================================
16. AUDIT BOUNDARY
==================================================

Audit and performance procedure and findings: CONTROL 07.

This CONTROL MAY consume an authorized CONTROL 07 finding as evidence or
a recovery-review trigger.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT rewrite audit standards, performance tiers,
Variance, or audit independence.

==================================================
17. PROCUREMENT AND SUCCESSOR PERFORMANCE
==================================================

CONTROL 12 — Competitive Node Procurement remains RESERVED, undrafted, and
without a live CONTROL file.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT define bidding, contractor selection, prime or
subcontractor structures, qualification, competition rules, takeover
rights, procurement scoring, or replacement-contractor selection.

This CONTROL MAY govern recovery from a contractor when a valid recovery
right exists. That fact SHALL NOT turn this CONTROL into procurement
doctrine.

Where another Node or contractor must complete failed work, this CONTROL
MAY recognize documented completion or corrective costs when they are
legally relevant to recoverable value.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT select the replacement provider or establish
procurement rules.

==================================================
18. PARKED, UNEXPENDED, AND SINKING-FUND AMOUNTS
==================================================

Where taxpayer-funded amounts remain unearned, unused, parked, reserved,
advanced, or otherwise subject to return under valid authority, this
CONTROL MAY govern their recovery disposition.

Cost/value treatment: CONTROL 06.
Property/pooling: CONTROL 00C.

Where recovered amounts originated from or are lawfully attributable to a
Sinking Fund Node, disposition SHALL remain traceable to the appropriate
lawful destination.

Sinking Fund Node ontology: CONTROL 01.
Sinking Fund vocabulary: CONTROL 02.
Sinking Fund cost treatment: CONTROL 06.

This CONTROL does not invent sinking-fund doctrine and SHALL NOT
redefine those homes.

==================================================
19. TRANSPARENCY
==================================================

Recovery status SHALL be capable of public accountability to the extent
permitted by law and appropriate privacy or security limitations.

API, schema, ledger, blockchain, digital-identity, database-design,
publication-protocol, and mobile-application mechanics: CONTROL 11.
CONTROL 11 remains reserved and is not drafted here.

This CONTROL establishes information and accountability requirements. It
does not create technical implementation.

==================================================
20. ENFORCEMENT
==================================================

Noncompliance with this CONTROL includes:

• Treating a Signal or audit finding as a final recovery determination
  without a lawful recovery basis
• Using this CONTROL as a pretext for unauthorized taking or confiscation
• Inventing clawback percentages or punitive multiplication as ordinary
  recovery
• Silently waiving, reducing, concealing, or extinguishing a recovery
  obligation
• Erasing recovery history upon Close
• Treating this CONTROL as Exit, audit procedure, Cost/Value definition,
  procurement, or Root authority
• Creating a tribunal, office, or enforcement power under this CONTROL

Noncompliance MAY be identified by:

• Authorized NBBF CONTROL stewardship under CONTROL 00
• Signals under CONTROL 00B
• Audit under CONTROL 07

Detailed audit procedure: CONTROL 07.
This CONTROL does not create an enforcement agency, tribunal, board,
criminal penalty schedule, or new governmental power.

23. NORTH STAR
==================================================

What government paid remains answerable.
What was not earned remains recoverable where law says so.
What cannot be shown is not taken.

No finding is a verdict by itself.
No settlement is real unless it can be seen.
No close erases the record.

delegated by CONTROL 00.
