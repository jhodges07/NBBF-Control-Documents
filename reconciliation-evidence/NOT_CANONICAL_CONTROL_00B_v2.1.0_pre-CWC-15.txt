00B_Signal_and_Non-Interference_Control

Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF)

Version: 2.1.0
Status: Authoritative Control Document
Classification: Foundational
Scope: System-wide signaling, reporting rights, signal integrity, evidence preservation, signal lifecycle, and non-interference operating rules.

Lineage:
This CONTROL is the Human Engineer-authorized merge of Signal v1.0 evidence
and Signal v2.1 evidence, executed under NBBF-CWC #5 pursuant to NBBF-CWC #4
decisions 1-C through 10-C. It is not a verbatim installation of either source.
Historical sources remain in reconciliation-evidence/ and are not live CONTROL
authority.

==================================================
GOVERNING AUTHORITY
==================================================

This document is issued pursuant to the NBBF Root Control Document
(00_Control.txt) and derives its authority therefrom.

This CONTROL is binding upon all NBBF nodes, owners, executive authorities,
auditors, contractors, participating organizations, and all derivative
implementations operating under NBBF authority.

Nothing within this document may be interpreted to restrict truthful reporting,
evidence preservation, or authorized oversight.

CONTROL 02 owns the definition of “Signal.”
This CONTROL owns Signal operating rules.
This CONTROL shall not redefine CONTROL 02.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes the signaling and non-interference operating rules
of the Node-Based Budget Framework.

Its purposes are to ensure that:

• Every funded promise creates observable expectations.
• Reality may always report back to authority.
• Failure cannot be hidden through bureaucracy.
• Evidence remains protected.
• Citizens retain visibility into authorized government activity.
• Truth remains structurally superior to bureaucracy.
• Taxpayer supremacy is preserved through ground-truth visibility.

Signals exist to compare lawful authorization with operational reality.

No funded node may exist without a protected mechanism through which reality
may report material variance.

==================================================
2. SIGNAL PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Reality is the Final Operational Authority.

Governmental process, organizational hierarchy, political preference,
institutional reputation, administrative convenience, or organizational
interests shall never override observable operational reality.

Every funded node creates:

• Obligations
• Observable outputs
• Measurable expectations
• Accountable ownership
• A protected signaling pathway

Every expectation creates the possibility of variance.

Every variance deserves a protected signal.

Silence, obstruction, or suppression of variance reporting constitutes
a structural failure of the system.

==================================================
3. REPORTING RIGHTS
==================================================

Humans legitimately affected by or downstream from a funded node possess:

• The right to know what was authorized.
• The right to observe actual performance.
• The right to report all material variance.
• The duty to report significant variance affecting public trust,
  constitutional authority, taxpayer value, safety, or operational readiness.

Reporting rights extend to every legitimate downstream participant including,
but not limited to:

• Government employees
• Officers
• Military personnel
• Contractors
• Auditors
• Inspectors General
• Vendors
• Citizens
• Taxpayers
• Oversight authorities

Reporting rights:

• Require no supervisory approval.
• Do not flow through management chains.
• May not be delayed.
• May not be filtered.
• May not be reclassified to suppress escalation.
• May not be conditioned upon organizational permission.

Reporting is a constitutional function of the system—not a grievance process.

==================================================
4. NON-INTERFERENCE
==================================================

No person, office, agency, node owner, executive authority, contractor,
authority, or system may:

• Block a report.
• Suppress a report.
• Alter a report.
• Improperly delay transmission.
• Conceal a report.
• Reclassify a report to avoid review.
• Destroy, substitute, or conceal evidence.
• Retaliate against the reporting party.
• Restrict lawful reporting.
• Reinterpret funded intent to avoid accountability.
• Improperly close a signal.
• Close a report without resolution at the appropriate node level.

Any attempt to interfere with signal integrity constitutes an independent
CONTROL violation.

Interfering actions are subject to immediate invalidation.

==================================================
5. SIGNAL OWNERSHIP
==================================================

Signals attach to nodes—not personalities, departments, administrations,
agencies, or political interests.

Each signal shall maintain, at minimum:

• Originating Node
• Reporter or origin, where lawfully appropriate
• Current Reviewing Authority
• Current Status
• Required Next Action
• Review Due Date
• Evidence References
• Escalation / Authority Chain
• Classification Level
• Resolution History
• Final Disposition

Signals become part of the permanent operational history of the node.

==================================================
6. SIGNAL LIFECYCLE
==================================================

Signals remain active until:

• The variance is resolved.
• The node is modified.
• The node is suspended.
• The node is terminated.
• A documented corrective action supersedes the open signal.

A documented corrective action may supersede an OPEN signal only when the
original signal, evidence, actions, and history remain permanently preserved.

CLOSE DOES NOT MEAN ERASE.

Supersession is not deletion, concealment, or alteration of the original record.

No signal may be buried through inaction.

Evidence attached to a signal shall never be altered, substituted,
concealed, or destroyed without preservation of the original record.

==================================================
7. SIGNAL REVIEW
==================================================

Signal review shall occur.

No signal shall remain indefinitely unreviewed.

CONTROL 05 owns review-calendar mechanics, including monthly operational
review and four-month legislative Decision-Node review.

This CONTROL shall not invent, restate, or replace that calendar.

An unresolved signal that passes its authorized review due date shall
automatically escalate.

Critical signals involving fraud, constitutional violations, significant
resource loss, safety, readiness, or other defined thresholds may bypass
ordinary sequencing where authorized by applicable NBBF CONTROLs.

==================================================
8. EXECUTION AUTHORITY
==================================================

Execution authority exists only within authorized scope.

Execution authority never includes authority to:

• Suppress signals.
• Alter evidence.
• Hide failure.
• Rewrite funded intent.
• Reinterpret funded intent to avoid accountability.
• Substitute procedure for delivery.
• Restrict lawful reporting.

Operational discretion exists only within funded, observable,
and constitutionally authorized boundaries.

==================================================
9. CLASSIFIED NODES
==================================================

Classified or restricted nodes remain fully subject to this CONTROL.

Classification may restrict visibility.

Classification shall never eliminate accountability.

Authorized reporting channels must include:

• House oversight
• Senate oversight
• Classified Inspectors General
• Other constitutionally authorized oversight authorities

Protected reporting channels shall remain available through those authorities.

Every classified node shall declare:

• Classification authority
• Classification duration
• Review requirement
• Declassification or unmasking event
• Reauthorization mechanism

Permanent secrecy is prohibited.

After the authorized secrecy period, the existence, duration, and authorized
cost range of the classified activity shall become visible unless reauthorized
through the CONTROL 05 / CONTROL 10 Decision-Node process.

This CONTROL does not invent the mechanics of CONTROL 05 or CONTROL 10.

==================================================
10. TAXPAYER VISIBILITY
==================================================

Taxpayer-funded activity shall not be insulated from lawful public observation
except where temporary constitutional restrictions are specifically authorized,
documented, and subject to review.

Transparency is the default.

Secrecy requires legitimate authority and justification.

CONTROL 11 owns publication, API, ledger, and digital transparency mechanics.

This CONTROL does not draft those mechanics.

==================================================
11. ENFORCEMENT
==================================================

Violation of this CONTROL automatically triggers:

• Immediate invalidation of interfering actions
• Audit review under CONTROL 07
• Preservation of evidence
• Oversight notification
• Authority review
• Escalation
• Possible suspension of node authority
• Review of node ownership or execution authority, including possible removal
• Other remedies authorized by the applicable CONTROLs

Clawback, exit, and recovery mechanics belong to CONTROL 00D, CONTROL 07,
and CONTROL 08 as applicable.

This CONTROL may require, trigger, or cross-reference those authorities.
This CONTROL shall not recreate their mechanics.

Retaliation against a reporting individual constitutes a structural offense,
not merely a personnel matter.

==================================================
12. DIGITAL REPRESENTATION
==================================================

Digital systems may represent, transport, inspect, or analyze signals.

Every signal shall be representable through structured metadata suitable for
lawful operational use.

The Constitutional Digital Twin (CDT) and UNBKE Runtime may be identified
as implementations.

No digital system, artificial intelligence system, API, ledger, runtime,
or automated process replaces human accountability or constitutional authority.

Machine representation shall never replace human accountability.

This CONTROL does not identify AGCL documents as NBBF CONTROLs.

CONTROL 11 owns public API, ledger, and publication mechanics.

==================================================
13. CONTROL NORTH STAR
==================================================

If the Republic funds it, reality must be allowed to speak.

Every promise creates expectations.

Every expectation permits measurement.

Every variance deserves a signal.

Every signal deserves protection.

No bureaucracy may silence truth.

No authority may outlive feedback.

No system may hide failure behind procedure.

Reality is the Final Operational Authority.

This document is a lock.
