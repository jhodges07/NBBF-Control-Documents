==================================================
GOVERNING AUTHORITY
==================================================

This document is the Root Control of the Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF).

All normative NBBF documents derive their authority from this document.

Where conflict exists between NBBF documents, this document prevails unless
explicitly amended through the NBBF amendment process.

This document establishes the constitutional engineering principles,
structural constraints, interpretation rules, and specification hierarchy
governing every component of the Node-Based Budget Framework.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

The purpose of the Node-Based Budget Framework is to engineer lawful
republican government so that public authority remains:

• Constitutional
• Human accountable
• Measurable
• Transparent
• Auditable
• Reviewable
• Recoverable
• Continuously aligned with taxpayer interests

NBBF exists to preserve accountability from legislative authorization to
the lowest human exercising operational judgment.

==================================================
2. WHAT NBBF IS
==================================================

The Node-Based Budget Framework is a constitutional engineering
specification.

It defines how lawful republican governments organize, authorize,
execute, measure, review, audit, and terminate public authority.

NBBF is jurisdiction-neutral.

The same engineering principles apply to:

• National government
• State government
• County government
• Municipal government
• Township government
• School districts
• Special districts
• Any other lawful republican governing body

==================================================
3. WHAT NBBF IS NOT
==================================================

NBBF is not:

• A political party
• A legislative proposal
• A budget
• A software application
• An accounting system
• A campaign platform
• A replacement for constitutional government

NBBF is an engineering specification.

==================================================
4. RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER STANDARDS
==================================================

NBBF is designed to complement related specifications.

AGCL establishes constitutional philosophy, taxpayer property rights,
authority limitations, monetary principles, and liberty-centered
governance.

NBBF establishes the engineering specification for organizing and
operating republican government.

The Constitutional Digital Twin (CDT) digitally represents NBBF
structures.

UNBKE provides knowledge engineering, evidence management,
traceability, and runtime knowledge support.

These standards remain independent while operating together.

==================================================
5. ENGINEERING AXIOMS
==================================================

The following axioms govern every normative NBBF document.

AXIOM 1

Reality disciplines authority.

AXIOM 2

Every lawful level of government functions as a republic.

AXIOM 3

Drift is the first enemy of liberty.

AXIOM 4

Authority exists to accomplish a defined public purpose.

AXIOM 5

Every exercise of public authority shall terminate in an identifiable
human owner capable of reasonably understanding, supervising,
defending, and answering for that responsibility.

Authority shall recursively decompose until reaching the lowest human
capable of directly exercising operational judgment.

AXIOM 6

Every human owner serves as both executor and guardian of public trust.

AXIOM 7

Failure shall become visible while still small enough to correct.

AXIOM 8

Structure precedes measurement.

Measurement precedes management.

AXIOM 9

Knowledge is distributed.

NBBF preserves lawful authority while allowing operational knowledge
to travel upward through protected signals.

AXIOM 10

Structure is superior to personalities.

AXIOM 11

Transparency is a system function.

AXIOM 12

Every public dollar must answer.

==================================================
6. THE SIX PILLARS
==================================================

The Node-Based Budget Framework is founded upon six permanent pillars.

Pillar One

Taxpayer Supremacy

Government exists to faithfully steward taxpayer resources.

--------------------------------------------------

Pillar Two

Recursive Human Accountability

Every public responsibility terminates in one accountable human owner.

--------------------------------------------------

Pillar Three

Reality Through Signals

Reality always possesses a protected path back to authority.

--------------------------------------------------

Pillar Four

Let's Fail

Failure shall become visible, measurable, localized,
recoverable, and educational.

--------------------------------------------------

Pillar Five

Digital Republic

Every lawful republic should be capable of faithful digital
representation without altering its constitutional structure.

--------------------------------------------------

Pillar Six

Continuous Stewardship

Authority, budgets, public resources, and responsibilities
shall undergo continuous operational review and periodic
legislative reauthorization.
