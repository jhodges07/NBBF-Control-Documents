Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF)
Master Specification Blueprint

Version: 0.1.0
Status: Internal Working Document — Non-Normative
Purpose: Define the architecture, document responsibilities, dependencies, naming conventions, review cadence, and publication structure of the Node-Based Budget Framework before canonical drafting.

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

This blueprint is the architect's drawing for the Node-Based Budget Framework.

It is not an authoritative NBBF control document.

It exists to prevent document overlap, terminology drift, contradictory rules,
uncontrolled expansion, and unclear ownership of concepts during development
of the canonical NBBF specification.

Every proposed NBBF requirement shall have one primary document home.

Documents may reference other documents.

Documents shall not silently redefine concepts owned elsewhere.

This blueprint governs the drafting process only.

======================================================================
2. WORKING DEFINITION OF NBBF
======================================================================

The Node-Based Budget Framework (NBBF) is a constitutional engineering
specification for organizing, authorizing, executing, measuring, auditing,
reviewing, and terminating public authority through accountable,
human-owned nodes at every lawful level of republican government.

NBBF is designed to preserve traceability from legislative authorization
to the lowest human exercising operational judgment.

======================================================================
3. PRIMARY DESIGN OBJECTIVES
======================================================================

NBBF shall be designed to accomplish the following:

1. Preserve Taxpayer Supremacy.

2. Maintain a continuous chain of human accountability from legislative
   authorization to operational execution.

3. Decompose authority until responsibility reaches the lowest human
   exercising direct judgment.

4. Ensure every public dollar remains traceable through accountable nodes.

5. Allow operational personnel to compare authorized resources and outcomes
   with operational reality.

6. Protect signals when authorization and reality do not match.

7. Detect drift before it becomes institutionalized.

8. Treat failure as expected information rather than something to conceal.

9. Separate legislative governance from operational management.

10. Require monthly operational review of applicable subordinate nodes.

11. Require legislative reauthorization, modification, reduction, or
    expiration of Decision Nodes at least every four months.

12. Function across national, state, county, municipal, township,
    school-district, special-district, and other lawful republican
    jurisdictions.

13. Preserve human responsibility regardless of automation, software,
    artificial intelligence, or organizational complexity.

14. Produce government structures that are measurable, auditable,
    transparent, and understandable to ordinary taxpayers.

======================================================================
4. FOUNDATIONAL ENGINEERING AXIOMS — WORKING SET
======================================================================

AXIOM 1 — REALITY DISCIPLINES AUTHORITY

Authority may authorize actions and resources.

Reality determines whether the authorized result occurred.

No administrative process, narrative, title, or hierarchy may substitute
for observable reality.


AXIOM 2 — REPUBLICAN GOVERNMENT EXISTS AT EVERY LAWFUL LEVEL

Every lawful governing jurisdiction operating under NBBF shall function
according to republican principles of representation, bounded authority,
public accountability, human responsibility, and rule-based governance.

The same structural principles shall scale from national government to
local governing bodies.


AXIOM 3 — DRIFT IS THE FIRST ENEMY OF LIBERTY

Every unmanaged system tends to drift in authority, cost, mission,
measurement, knowledge, ownership, and accountability.

NBBF shall continuously expose and correct drift.


AXIOM 4 — AUTHORITY EXISTS TO ACCOMPLISH A DEFINED PURPOSE

Public authority shall exist for declared purposes and shall not perpetuate
itself merely because it previously existed.


AXIOM 5 — RECURSIVE HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY

Every node shall have one identifiable human owner.

Authority and responsibility shall recursively decompose through subordinate
nodes until every executable responsibility reaches the lowest human capable
of directly exercising judgment over that responsibility.

Every human owner shall be capable of reasonably understanding, supervising,
defending, and answering for the node and subordinate nodes within that
owner's span of accountability.

When responsibility exceeds reasonable human capacity, the structure shall
decompose further.

No public responsibility may terminate in a committee, agency, algorithm,
software system, artificial intelligence system, or anonymous organization.


AXIOM 6 — EVERY HUMAN OWNER IS BOTH EXECUTOR AND GUARDIAN

Every human owner shall compare lawful authorization with operational reality.

Material variance shall generate a protected signal.

The lowest operational owner possesses the same duty to question missing,
excess, misdirected, or unusable resources as higher authorities.


AXIOM 7 — LET'S FAIL

Failure shall be expected.

Systems shall therefore be designed so failure becomes visible, bounded,
traceable, recoverable, and educational.

Hidden failure is more dangerous than acknowledged failure.


AXIOM 8 — MEASUREMENT REQUIRES STRUCTURE

What cannot be defined cannot be reliably measured.

What cannot be measured cannot be responsibly managed.

NBBF therefore establishes structure before measurement.


AXIOM 9 — KNOWLEDGE IS DISTRIBUTED

No legislature, executive, agency, manager, expert, or information system
possesses all relevant knowledge.

NBBF shall preserve lawful downward authority while allowing operational
knowledge and signals to travel upward.


AXIOM 10 — STRUCTURE OUTLASTS PERSONALITIES

NBBF shall rely on structural controls rather than confidence in particular
politicians, administrators, parties, or personalities.


AXIOM 11 — TRANSPARENCY IS A SYSTEM FUNCTION

Public accountability shall arise from the structure of the system rather
than depending upon scandals, investigative journalism, whistleblowers,
or political initiative.


AXIOM 12 — EVERY PUBLIC DOLLAR MUST ANSWER

Every public dollar shall be attributable to lawful authorization,
an accountable path, a responsible human owner, a declared purpose,
and an observable disposition.

5. GOVERNANCE CADENCE
======================================================================

NBBF separates operational management from legislative governance.

--------------------------------------------------
5.1 Monthly Operational Review
--------------------------------------------------

Applicable non-legislative nodes shall be reviewed at least monthly by
their immediate accountable parent or designated reviewing authority.

Monthly review may include:

- Authorized resources versus resources received
- Budget versus actual expenditure
- Performance
- Readiness
- Material variance
- Unresolved signals
- Staffing
- Equipment
- Deliverables
- Risks
- Corrective actions

Monthly review does not require legislative action.


--------------------------------------------------
5.2 Immediate Signal Escalation
--------------------------------------------------

Material variance shall not wait for a monthly review.

Signals involving significant shortages, excess resources, fraud,
safety, constitutional conflict, operational failure, or other declared
thresholds shall escalate according to their applicable signal rules
when detected.


--------------------------------------------------
5.3 Four-Month Legislative Review / Die Date
--------------------------------------------------

Decision Nodes requiring legislative authority shall reach a formal review
point no later than every four months.

At that point the legislative authority shall affirmatively choose among:

- Reauthorize
- Modify
- Reduce
- Restructure
- Suspend
- Terminate

Failure to affirmatively continue authority shall invoke the applicable
expiration or Die Date rule.

The four-month review is an authority decision.

It is not legislative micromanagement of subordinate operational nodes.

6. DOCUMENT CLASSIFICATION
======================================================================

Every NBBF document shall belong primarily to one of four classes.

FOUNDATIONAL

Defines governing principles, ontology, vocabulary, and system-wide
constraints.

STRUCTURAL

Defines relationships among jurisdictions, nodes, authority paths,
reporting paths, and system architecture.

OPERATIONAL

Defines recurring execution, review, budgeting, audit, reporting,
and lifecycle processes.

INFORMATIVE

Explains philosophy, intellectual lineage, examples, implementation guidance,
publication practices, and educational material.

Informative documents create no authority unless explicitly incorporated
by a normative document.

======================================================================

    |
    | Legislative Decision Node
    v

    |
    v

    v

    |
    v

    |
    v
Platoon
    |
    v
Squad
    |
    v
Fire Team
    |
    v
Fire Team Leader
    |
    v
Direct operational responsibility

The Fire Team Leader does not report directly to Congress.

The Fire Team Leader does possess the right and duty to ask:

"Where is the equipment that was authorized for this node?"

while retaining traceability to the original authorization.

This same principle shall apply to civilian government.

13. NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
======================================================================

Normative documents should use controlled language.

SHALL
Mandatory requirement.

SHALL NOT
Mandatory prohibition.

MAY
Permitted action.

SHOULD
Preferred practice requiring justification if not followed.

INFORMATIVE
Explanation only.

Terms such as "might," "probably," "hopefully," and similar ambiguous
language should not appear in normative requirements.

PATCH
Corrects wording, spelling, formatting, or non-substantive error.

Examples:

2.0.0
Initial canonical Version 2 release.

2.1.0
Adds a compatible review mechanism.

2.1.1
Corrects wording without changing meaning.

17. CROSS-REFERENCE RULE
======================================================================

A document may reference another document.

It shall not duplicate an authoritative rule merely for convenience.

Example:

from 01_Node_Ontology.

It shall not create a second incompatible Node definition.

Where conflict exists:

00_Control prevails over all other NBBF documents.

02_Definitions_and_Terms controls vocabulary unless explicitly superseded
by 00_Control.

Specialized documents govern their assigned subject area only within
the boundaries created by higher-order documents.

20. RELATIONSHIP TO AGCL
======================================================================

NBBF and AGCL are separate standards.

AGCL addresses broader constitutional philosophy, liberty, property,
taxation, monetary integrity, authority limitations, and related principles.

NBBF addresses the engineering of republican budgeting, execution,
accountability, performance, reporting, and public-resource stewardship.

NBBF may reference AGCL where applicable.

NBBF shall not silently absorb or duplicate AGCL controls.

Any future integration shall be explicit and versioned.

======================================================================
