AUTHORITATIVE HOME
==================================================

This CONTROL answers:

WHEN DOES AUTHORITY REVIEW OCCUR, WHEN IS IT DUE, AND HOW IS
REAUTHORIZATION DISPOSED?

The following are not the same event:

A. AUDIT / PERFORMANCE FINDING — CONTROL 07
B. AUTHORITY REVIEW — this CONTROL
C. REAUTHORIZATION DECISION — this CONTROL, made through lawful
   human / Decision-Node authority

This CONTROL determines when scheduled review occurs and how authorized
inputs are considered in the reauthorization process.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT become a second audit CONTROL, Signal CONTROL,
recovery CONTROL, Decision-Node CONTROL, or API/publication CONTROL.

Concise vocabulary: CONTROL 02.
Node ontology: CONTROL 01.

==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes the Authority Review and Reauthorization Cycle
of NBBF.

Drift is the first enemy of liberty.

Authority, budgets, public resources, and responsibilities SHALL undergo
continuous operational review and periodic reauthorization.

Periodic review SHALL NOT become an excuse for permanent bureaucracy.
The purpose is measurable stewardship and recurring justification of
delegated authority.

Every material review SHALL be capable of answering:

• What was authorized?
• What actually occurred?
• What value was produced?
• What materially varied?
• What Signals remain unresolved?
• What corrective actions occurred?
• What authority should continue?
• What authority should change?
• What authority should end?

==================================================
2. FOUR-MONTH AND OPERATIONAL REVIEW CADENCE
==================================================

Where NBBF applies, funded Nodes SHALL be subject to a four-month
authority and performance review cycle unless a more specific lawful
authorization establishes a different review period that remains
identifiable and no less accountable.

CONTROL 00B already requires Signal review and attributes to this CONTROL
both monthly operational review and four-month legislative Decision-Node
review. This CONTROL owns that calendar. It SHALL NOT rewrite CONTROL 00B
Signal-review operating rules.

A. MONTHLY OPERATIONAL REVIEW
   Recurring operational review of open Signals, performance indicators,
   and other authorized operational inputs. This is not an audit
   procedure.

B. FOUR-MONTH AUTHORITY / PERFORMANCE REVIEW
   Periodic authority review and reauthorization consideration for the
   Node, program, or other authorized activity.

Neither event is a CONTROL 07 audit. CONTROL 07 supplies audit and
performance findings. CONTROL 06 supplies cost/value information.
CONTROL 00B supplies Signal information.

Legislative Decision-Node interface mechanics for conducting a
Decision-Node reauthorization: CONTROL 10. CONTROL 10 remains reserved
and is not drafted here.

==================================================
3. REVIEW CLOCK
==================================================

Each review cycle SHALL be capable of identifying:

• Review-cycle start
• Review-due date
• Review period
• Timely review
• Overdue review
• Documented disposition
• Next review date
• Permanent review history

The review-due date is the date by which documented review disposition
SHALL occur.

A review is timely when disposition is documented on or before the
review-due date.

A review is overdue when the review-due date has passed without
documented disposition.

This CONTROL does not invent software, APIs, endpoints, schemas, or
publication protocols. Machine and publication mechanics: CONTROL 11,
reserved.

==================================================
4. MISSED REVIEW
==================================================

A missed review is an overdue review.

CONTROL 00B states that an unresolved Signal that passes its authorized
review due date SHALL automatically escalate. This CONTROL owns the
calendar that makes that due date meaningful. This CONTROL SHALL NOT
rewrite CONTROL 00B.

A missed review SHALL NOT silently extend authority forever.

Silence, inaction, automated continuation, hidden Signals, or delayed
audit SHALL NOT equal reauthorization.

Missed review is an NBBF noncompliance and escalation condition. It does
not, by itself, repeal constitutional or statutory authority.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT invent an automatic governmental shutdown where
superior law would prohibit it.

Where superior law does not require otherwise, continued NBBF Node
exercise of authority after a missed review is fail-closed as to NBBF
legitimacy: it is unauthorized NBBF continuation until a documented
disposition under §6 occurs.

==================================================
5. REAUTHORIZATION
==================================================

Reauthorization is an attributable human or governmental decision made
through lawful authority.

Reauthorization SHALL NOT occur merely because:

• Time passed
• Nobody objected
• Software automatically renewed it
• A budget continued
• A contractor continued working
• An administrator failed to act
• A Signal was hidden
• An audit was delayed

Silence SHALL NOT equal consent or reauthorization.

Every material reauthorization SHALL be attributable to the lawful human
or Decision-Node authority responsible for it.

Human Owner meaning: CONTROL 01.
Republican application of Decision Nodes: CONTROL 03.
Legislative Decision-Node interface: CONTROL 10, reserved.

No automated system MAY silently reauthorize governmental authority.

==================================================
6. REVIEW DISPOSITIONS
==================================================

A completed authority review SHALL record one of the following
dispositions, or an equivalent disposition that maps unambiguously to
one of them:

• REAUTHORIZE
• REAUTHORIZE WITH DOCUMENTED CORRECTIVE CONDITIONS
• MODIFY WITHIN EXISTING LAWFUL AUTHORITY
• REFER FOR FURTHER AUDIT / REVIEW
• REFER FOR EXIT / DISENGAGEMENT REVIEW
• REFER FOR RECOVERY REVIEW
• DO NOT REAUTHORIZE
• REVIEW INCOMPLETE / AUTHORITY UNRESOLVED

A disposition MAY trigger another authoritative CONTROL. It SHALL NOT
absorb that CONTROL’s mechanics.

• Further audit / performance: CONTROL 07
• Exit / Disengagement: CONTROL 00D
• Recovery: CONTROL 08
• Signal treatment: CONTROL 00B
• Legislative Decision-Node process: CONTROL 10, reserved

This CONTROL does not invent punitive powers, penalties, tribunals, or
offices.

DO NOT REAUTHORIZE is a review disposition. Operating Exit and
Disengagement remain CONTROL 00D.

REVIEW INCOMPLETE / AUTHORITY UNRESOLVED is not reauthorization.

==================================================
8. HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY
==================================================

Every material review disposition SHALL be attributable to accountable
human authority consistent with CONTROL 01 and applicable constitutional
or statutory authority.

CDT, UNBKE, future Republican Digital Twin implementations, or other
software MAY assist with scheduling, reminders, evidence aggregation,
telemetry, visualization, recording, or publication.

They SHALL NOT replace lawful human accountability or create authority.

Implementation of publication, API, and ledger mechanics: CONTROL 11,
reserved. This CONTROL does not draft those mechanics.

==================================================
9. CLASSIFIED AND RESTRICTED MATTERS
==================================================

Review obligations do not disappear merely because information is
classified or lawfully restricted.

Classified Signal operating rules: CONTROL 00B. This CONTROL SHALL NOT
duplicate those rules.

Classification may restrict visibility. It SHALL NOT eliminate the
review-due date, the requirement of documented disposition, or
accountability.

Where reauthorization of continued secrecy is implicated, CONTROL 00B
requires Decision-Node process involving this CONTROL and CONTROL 10.
This CONTROL owns the review calendar for that reauthorization. CONTROL
10 remains reserved for Decision-Node interface mechanics and is not
drafted here.

==================================================
10. REVIEW HISTORY
==================================================

Review history SHALL be permanent and traceable.

A later reauthorization SHALL NOT erase:

• Prior failures
• Signals
• Audit findings
• Recovery findings
• Corrective actions
• Previous review dispositions
• Previous authority decisions

Close is not erase.

Compatible principle: CONTROL 00B CLOSE DOES NOT MEAN ERASE. This CONTROL
does not duplicate Signal-close operating doctrine.

Ledger, API, and publication mechanics: CONTROL 11, reserved.

==================================================
11. TAXPAYER VISIBILITY
==================================================

Review status and lawful review outcomes SHOULD be visible to taxpayers
to the maximum extent permitted by constitutional or statutory authority.

Publication, API, ledger, blockchain, database, identity, and
mobile-application mechanics: CONTROL 11, reserved.

This CONTROL states the governance principle. It does not create
endpoints, schemas, or publication protocols.

==================================================
15. ENFORCEMENT
==================================================

Noncompliance with this CONTROL includes:

• Treating time, silence, software, budget continuation, contractor
  continuation, hidden Signals, or delayed audit as reauthorization
• Missing a review-due date without documented disposition
• Silently extending NBBF Node authority after a missed review
• Erasing review history upon later reauthorization
• Treating this CONTROL as audit procedure, Signal operations, Exit,
  recovery, procurement, or Root authority
• Using automation to silently reauthorize governmental authority

Noncompliance MAY be identified by:

• Authorized NBBF CONTROL stewardship under CONTROL 00
• Signals under CONTROL 00B, including auto-escalation of unresolved
  Signals past their authorized review due date
• Audit under CONTROL 07

Resulting referrals MAY be made to CONTROL 00D, CONTROL 07, or CONTROL 08
as applicable.

Detailed audit procedure: CONTROL 07.
This CONTROL does not create an enforcement agency, tribunal, board,
criminal penalty schedule, or new governmental power.

18. NORTH STAR
==================================================
